We're excited to share powerful enhancements to Health Data & Trends, plus workflow improvements that make coaching more efficient and intuitive:

- **Enhanced Fitness & Recovery Tracking:** We've expanded our Health Data & Trends capabilities with two critical metrics. Respiratory Rate now appears in your HRV Trends Graph, giving you deeper insight into client recovery and nervous system regulation. We've also added VO2 Max tracking to what is now the "Fitness" graph (formerly Resting Heart Rate Trends), providing a more complete picture of your clients' cardiovascular fitness and endurance capacity over time.

- **Client Insights Dashboard:** A new Client Insights tab on your Dashboard consolidates key information about client progress, trends, and important markers in one centralized view. Access the metrics that matter most without navigating between multiple screens, helping you make faster, more informed coaching decisions.

- **Filtered Exercise Selection:** Program design just got faster. Filter your exercise library by movement pattern or equipment during the program building process, making it easier to find the right exercises that match your programming intent. Whether you're building around available equipment or targeting specific movement patterns, this feature streamlines exercise selection and keeps your design process flowing.

- **Voice Notes in Messaging & Comments:** Communicate with clients in a more personal, efficient way. Record and send voice messages directly within client conversations and workout comments, perfect for providing detailed feedback, explaining complex concepts, or simply adding a human touch to your digital coaching. Voice notes make remote coaching feel more connected while saving you time on lengthy typed responses.

- **Duplicate Forms:** Coaches can now duplicate existing forms directly in the Index, making it faster to create variations of your intake forms, assessments, or check-in questionnaires. Build on what works without starting from scratch every time you need a customized form.

- **Program Source Visibility:** When workouts are assigned from a program template, we now display the source program name on both coach dashboards and client calendars. This transparency helps you track program adherence and makes it easier for clients to understand their training structure and progression.

- **Movement Patterns & Equipment in Demo Videos:** Client calendars now display movement patterns and required equipment alongside demo videos, helping clients prepare for workouts more effectively and understand the training focus before they begin.

- **Streamlined Index UI & Quick Workout Access:** We've refined buttons and navigation throughout the Index for a cleaner, more intuitive experience. We've also moved the Start Workout button back to the top of client workout views, making it faster for clients to begin their training sessions.
