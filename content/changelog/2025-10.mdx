We have an epic feature release days away. The team has been hard at work to bring you:

- **Seamless Health Data & Wearables Integration:** Connect your client's entire health ecosystem automatically. CoachRx now syncs data from Apple Health, WHOOP, Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, and more. No more chasing compliance data or relying on subjective client reporting. Focus your energy on insights and breakthrough coaching moments backed by objective metrics.

The dashboard highlights patterns and correlations that inform your coaching decisions and create meaningful client conversations.

- **Smart Auto-Tracking Control:** Automatic data sync for each client regardless of prescribed lifestyle focus. Precision without hassle, giving you coaching intelligence that transforms how you monitor and analyze client progress.
