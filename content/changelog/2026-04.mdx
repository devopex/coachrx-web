Welcome to your CoachRx March digest! We're excited to share what's new, noteworthy, and next for enhancing your coaching experience. Let's dive in!

### Platform Updates: What's New

We're excited to share a dedicated Conditioning Library, expanded client autonomy, and email-based workflow improvements designed to streamline your programming and communication:

- **Conditioning Library:** Mixed Modal is now Conditioning, and we've built a complete library system around it. Save your conditioning flows for quick reuse, browse and search your saved workouts in a dedicated library view, and manage all your conditioning programming from one place. New accounts now come pre-loaded with conditioning templates to help you get started immediately, no need to build from scratch.

- **Client-Side Workout Editing:** Clients can now edit workouts you've assigned to them, giving you more flexibility in how you collaborate. You maintain complete control over who gets this capability, but for clients who need to make real-time adjustments or log additional work, they can now do so without leaving the platform. Perfect for coaches who want to empower mature clients while maintaining visibility over their training.

- **Reply to Comments via Email:** Stop switching between your inbox and the platform. Coaches and clients can now reply directly to comment notification emails instead of opening the app. Your response flows seamlessly into the conversation thread, keeping communication moving without disrupting your workflow.

- **Improved Workout Builder Design:** Cleaner action icons with hover effects and tooltips create a more polished editing experience. Calendar exercises now show a grab cursor so it's immediately clear they can be reordered, small details that make programming faster and more intuitive.

- **Tag-Based Activity Feed Filtering:** Filter your activity feed by client tags to focus on specific groups. Whether you're checking in on a particular training cohort, program type, or priority group, you can now zero in on exactly who you need to see.

- **Profile Hover Card in Docked Chat:** Hovering over a client's avatar in the docked chat now shows their full profile card, instant access to client context without leaving your conversation.

- **Resend Invitation Button:** No more hunting for invitation links. Coaches can now resend pending invitations directly from the client's "Invite Pending" label with a single click.

- **Exact Match Toggle for Exercise History:** Search for exact exercise names in history instead of partial matches (available on web and mobile). Perfect for when you need precise data for "Back Squat" without pulling up every squat variation in your library.

- **YouTube/Vimeo Timestamp Support:** Embedded videos can now start at a specific timestamp, ideal for directing clients to the exact moment in a technique video or educational content.

- **Terra Integration Optimization:** We've reduced WHOOP API usage through optimized wearable data syncing, keeping us within WHOOP rate limits and improving sync reliability for all Terra-connected devices.

- **TrainHeroic Transition Assistance:** Improved migration tools for coaches moving from TrainHeroic to CoachRx, making the switch smoother and less disruptive to your business.

- **Stripe Connect Migration Support:** Organizations can now create a Standard Stripe account while keeping their existing Express account active during migration giving you control over your transition timeline.

### Performance Improvements

- **Platform-Wide Speed Gains:** Loading conversations, clients, exercise history, and the exercise library are all significantly faster. We've optimized queries and data handling across the platform for a more responsive experience, especially noticeable for coaches with large rosters and extensive exercise libraries.
