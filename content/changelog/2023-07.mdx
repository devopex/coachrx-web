### CALENDAR & LIFESTYLE RX PERFORMANCE UPGRADES

Refinement, Bug Fix

This month the development team made significant refinements and fixes to the client program design calendar, lifestyle Rx feature, and exercise history.

- **Client Calendar Refinements:** Coaches will always land on the current week at the top of the screen, notice a UI update to include a skeleton, and experience faster loading times thanks to performance upgrades and dummy icons in place where loading times cannot be minimized.

- **Lifestyle Rx Refinements:** Improved lifestyle prescription performance and speed.

- **Lifestyle Index Bug:** Fixed internal server error when prescribing lifestyle from the lifestyle programs index.

- **Exercise History Bug:** Fixed ordering of exercise history to appear chronologically.
