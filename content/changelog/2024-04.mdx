We're excited to share some fantastic updates and improvements we've rolled out to both the CoachRx Web App and the Client Mobile App. These enhancements are designed to make your coaching experience smoother and more intuitive.

Here's what's new:

Web App Enhancements:

- **Docked Chat Window:** Opening a chat from the messages panel now displays as a docked chat on your current page, improving multitasking without switching to a full-screen messages page.

- **RxBot Private Beta:** We've introduced the private beta of RxBot, your soon to be new AI coaching assistant improving your program design and freeing up time for more coaching.

- **Enhanced Messaging Performance:** Improved efficiency when sending messages to multiple clients simultaneously.

- **Infinite Scrolling in Client Calendars:** Say goodbye to the hassle of flipping calendar months. Enjoy seamless loading of weeks as you scroll.

- **Client Streaks Introduced:** Motivate your clients with our new Streaks feature, boosting engagement and consistency.

- **Performance Improvements and Bug Fixes:** Various tweaks and fixes to enhance app stability and performance.

Client Mobile App Updates:

- **Enhanced Navigation:** Clients can now swipe on the day of the week header to switch between weeks or on a day's Rx to move between days, enhancing ease of use.

These updates are part of our commitment to continuously improving and providing you with the best tools to enhance your coaching effectiveness. Dive in, explore the new features, and as always, we appreciate your feedback to make CoachRx even better.

Stay tuned for more updates and thanks for being a vital part of our coaching community!

Best, The CoachRx Team

Our team has been hard at work making improvements to both the Coach web app and the Client mobile app. These enhancements are designed to streamline your workflow, boost client engagement, and ultimately support the incredible work you do every day.

Here’s what’s new:

- **For Coaches:** Web App Enhancements

The infinite scrolling calendar and readability enhancements are designed to make your workflow as efficient and pleasant as possible. Infinite scroll will be rolling out to all users over the next few weeks.

- **Infinite Scrolling Calendar:** We’ve introduced an infinite scrolling feature to your calendar to make programming for your clients smoother and more efficient than ever before. Say goodbye to clicking back and forth between months and hello to seamless navigation through your programming tasks.

- **Color and Font Enhancements:** We understand how important readability is for your daily tasks. That’s why we’ve made adjustments to the colors and fonts across the Coach web app. These enhancements are designed to reduce eye strain and improve your overall experience when planning and reviewing programs.

- **For Clients:** Mobile App Updates

The Streaks feature introduces a new layer of motivation. By turning consistency into a game, clients are more likely to remain engaged with their training, leading to better outcomes and a stronger coach-client relationship.

- **Streaks:** To keep your clients motivated and engaged, we’ve launched the Streaks feature in the Client mobile app. This gamification element rewards clients for consistency, creating a sense of excitement and achievement with each completed workout. It’s a fun way to encourage them to stay on track and reach their fitness goals.

Our goal at CoachRx is to provide you and your clients with the best possible tools to succeed. We’re excited to see how these updates enhance your coaching practice and your clients' experiences. As always, we value your feedback and look forward to hearing how these changes are making a difference for you. Stay tuned for more updates, and thank you for being a part of the CoachRx community!
