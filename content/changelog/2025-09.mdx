We're constantly working to enhance your CoachRx experience. Here's what's new this month:

- **Mobile Workout Management:** Coaches can now move workouts directly from the mobile app, giving you the flexibility to adjust client schedules on the go. Perfect for those busy coaching days when you need to make quick adjustments from anywhere. Client Workout Feedback: When clients move their workouts, they're now prompted to share why they're rescheduling (optional). This valuable insight helps coaches better understand their clients' needs and scheduling challenges. Coach Message Management: Coaches can now unsend messages directly from the web app, providing better control over communication and the ability to correct any mistakes or clarifications needed. Smart Message Notifications: Message notification emails now include a 3-minute delay to account for coaches who may unsend messages, reducing unnecessary email clutter for your clients. Multiple Chat Windows: Stay connected with multiple clients simultaneously! You can now keep several chat conversations open at once, making it easier to manage ongoing conversations throughout your coaching day.
