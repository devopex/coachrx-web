We're constantly working to enhance your CoachRx experience. Here's what's new this month:

Big News

- **Macro/Calorie Calculator Launch:** Our powerful new built-in calculator makes nutrition programming effortless, allowing you to generate personalized macro recommendations and apply them directly to client calendars in seconds. Watch the demo above and learn more here .

Platform Updates

- **Enhanced Lifestyle Rx:** Building on our April release, we've continued to optimize this powerful new feature based on your feedback.

- **Bulk Edit/Delete for Lifestyle Rx:** You can now edit or delete multiple recurring Lifestyle Rx items at once, saving you time when managing client prescriptions that were created through the repetition feature.

- **Exercise Calendar Design Refresh:** We've completely revamped the exercise calendar interface for improved visual clarity, easier navigation, and better program visualization. This is still in beta and you can turn that on in your settings.

- **Performance Improvements and Optimizations:** We've made numerous behind-the-scenes enhancements to speed up the platform and improve your overall experience.

Stay tuned for more exciting updates coming in June! Wearables integration is in development and coming soon.

Remember, your input shapes the future of CoachRx. Keep those suggestions coming! Thank you for being part of the journey!
