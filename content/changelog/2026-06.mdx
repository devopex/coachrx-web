#### Every month, your coaching OS gets a little sharper. June was about taking friction out of the things you do every day: getting the right demo in front of a client, moving programs around the calendar, and keeping the whole conversation in one place. Here is what shipped.

- **Platform Updates:** What's New

#### Assess

MyMacros+ via Terra. Nutrition data from MyMacros+ now syncs through Terra, keeping the food side of coaching connected to everything else.

WHOOP fixes. We resolved the outstanding WHOOP connection issues, so wearable data flows the way it should.

#### Consult

Upload your own demo videos. Not every movement you coach lives on YouTube. Upload your own exercise demos directly, right alongside the YouTube and Vimeo links you already use, so clients see exactly how you want it done.

Drag & drop into comments and chat. When a clip or screenshot explains a fix faster than a paragraph, drag and drop videos, images, and files straight into comments and chat.

Reply by email. Clients can now reply directly to your comment and message email notifications, so a conversation keeps moving even when they are away from the app.

#### Design

Assign programs from the client calendar. A new action lets you assign a program straight from the client calendar, without leaving the view you are already in.

Search workouts by title. Find the session you are looking for by searching workout titles directly on the coach calendar.

Copy & paste across clients. Pasting workout items now correctly resolves linked metrics across clients and programs, so reusing your best work no longer breaks the data behind it.

Drag to reorder metrics. Reorder metrics in the client sidebar and index templates by dragging them where they belong.

More visible coach notes. Your notes now stand out clearly on client calendars, so the context you leave is the context they see.

Training load in notifications and feeds. Workout notification details and dashboard feeds now show training load, so you read intensity at a glance instead of opening the session to check.

#### Operate

International currencies. Billing now supports currencies that are not denominated in cents, making CoachRx work cleanly for more coaches around the world.

Other improvements & fixes

- **Mobile connectivity alert:** clients are warned when they lose their network connection. Optional weight logging on mobile: weight entry is no longer required. UI consistency: unified input fields, dropdowns, and settings panels across the app. Performance: bug fixes and speed improvements.
