### MOBILE APP V2 iOS BETA

New Feature

Launch of V2 mobile app for iOS users (one app for coaches and clients). Read this article for more information and how to join the beta test.

For Clients:

From the Daily Rx screen, you can seamlessly fill out exercise and lifestyle prescriptions and complete a weekly check-in to share an overview of your training, nutrition, and lifestyle quality for the week. CoachRx makes it easy to update account information, handle billing, schedule consultations, and communicate with your coach as needed. In the Client Corner, you can adjust your training schedule, fill out equipment lists, complete an intake form, view important documents, and track your progress with visual graphs for resistance and energy systems assessments. You can also view the most important success metric—compliance to their exercise and lifestyle prescriptions.

For Coaches:

Designed for delivering both personalized coaching and group programs, CoachRx has all of the professional coach’s needs covered. With a simple toggle from coach to client mode, you can bounce between logging their own workouts and communicating with your clients. Get a quick view of your client list, update their account details and billing, and review their training and lifestyle. From the Dashboard, you can view and comment on workouts, lifestyle, and weekly check-ins as they’re completed, plus get alerted by any Rx’s that are due. Relationships are at the heart of a great coaching service, so our communication tools—from the ability to send real-time messages (including videos) to receiving push notifications—are front and center.
