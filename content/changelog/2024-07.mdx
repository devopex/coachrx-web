We’re excited to share the latest updates and enhancements to both the CoachRx Web App and Mobile App. These improvements are designed to make your coaching experience more efficient and enjoyable.

Here’s What’s New in July:

Mobile App Enhancements:

- **Coach App Experience Redesign:** Stay connected and engaged with your clients no matter where you are. Our improved coach interface in light mode makes it easier than ever to:

📲 Message Clients 💬 Comment on Workouts 🔔 View Notifications 📊 View & Enter Workout Results 😍 Use Emoji Reactions

Minor Updates & Performance Improvements:

Improved client list search to allow spaces when searching for a client subscription. Improved weekly check-in question order.
