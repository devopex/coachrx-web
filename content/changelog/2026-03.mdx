We're excited to share a complete messaging overhaul, powerful mobile capabilities, and workflow enhancements designed to help you coach more efficiently and stay connected with your clients:

- **RxBot Communication Tools (Closed Beta):** AI-powered coaching assistance to help you communicate faster and more effectively. Get one-click workout feedback with three varied AI-generated suggestions (encouragement, feedback, or progression guidance). Generate context-aware messages from your Dashboard and Needs Attention views based on client health data. Customize RxBot's tone, message length, greeting style, and name usage preferences with a live preview. These tools amplify your coaching voice while saving valuable time.

- **Mobile:** Edit Workout & Client Autonomy (Closed Beta): Full workout editing capabilities now available on mobile for both coaches and clients. Add or edit warmups and cooldowns, swap and edit exercises with improved navigation, view exercise descriptions and demo videos, and communicate with the GIF picker. Coaches can edit workout titles and delete any workout, while clients can delete self-created workouts. Perfect for coaching on the go and giving clients controlled autonomy when life requires adjustments.

- **Conversations Overhaul:** We've redesigned how you communicate with clients. Messages now group together with iMessage-style bubbles when sent within 5 minutes of each other, creating a more natural conversation flow. The full-screen conversation view includes a new info sidebar showing client stats, compliance data, health insights, and goals right alongside your chat giving you complete context without leaving the conversation. For coaches with large rosters, we've added infinite scroll with server-side search and dramatically improved performance.

- **Workout Builder Redesign:** A complete visual refresh with cleaner icons, improved dark mode, hover-to-reveal actions, better visual hierarchy, and new superset colors. The redesign makes programming faster and more intuitive while maintaining all the power you need for Individual Design.

- **Workout Deep Links:** Every workout now has its own shareable URL. Opening a workout updates your browser URL, and direct links load the calendar at the correct date with the workout modal open. Full browser history support means you can navigate back and forth through workouts seamlessly, perfect for referencing past sessions or sharing specific workouts with clients.

- **Enhanced Client Onboarding:** Celebrate new client additions with a modal showing their invitation link, improved contextual help for coaching services settings, and automatic selection of your default intake form. New clients now get a workout tour on their first visit, setting them up for success from day one.

- **Notification Navigation:** Navigate between notifications using arrow buttons and keyboard shortcuts (left/right arrows) without closing the modal. A counter shows your position ("X of Y"), and notifications automatically mark as read while you browse helping you stay on top of your communication queue efficiently.

- **Dispute Notifications:** Stay informed when clients file payment disputes. Receive email alerts, in-app notifications, and visual badge indicators. Clicking a dispute notification routes you directly to the dispute details for immediate action.

- **Redesigned Pages:** Fresh, responsive layouts for Integrations, Partners, and Programs pages that match our latest design standards and provide a more intuitive experience across all screen sizes.

- **Consultations Sidebar:** View consultations and weekly check-in previews directly in the sidebar without leaving your current view, faster access to the information you need when planning client sessions.

- **Quick Task Creation:** Add Task icon now appears on client hover cards, letting you create tasks for clients instantly without navigating away from your current workflow.

- **Improved Weekly Check-in Display:** Cleaner, more readable weekly check-in cards on the client calendar give you faster insight into client responses and engagement patterns.

#### Performance Improvements

- **Activity Feed:** Up to 10x faster for coaches with large client rosters through optimized queries and lateral joins. Tabs now lazy-load with hover prefetch for instant responsiveness.

- **Client Loading:** Significantly improved performance when fetching and rendering clients throughout the platform.

- **Conversations:** Paginated API (20 messages per page) replaces loading all messages at once, especially helpful for coaches with extensive client message histories.

- **Duplicate Request Prevention:** Eliminated redundant API calls throughout the platform for a faster, smoother experience.
