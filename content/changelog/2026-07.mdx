This month, CoachRx gets easier to make your own. July brought custom theming across your emails and the mobile app, a smoother path for coaches moving over from another platform, and a long list of refinements across how you assess, consult, and design. Here is what shipped, organized the way you coach.

#### What Shipped in July

#### Assess

- **Metric ordering respected:** The client app now follows your custom metric group and metric ordering, so what clients see matches the way you organized it.

- **More accurate wearable weight:** Body weight now syncs reliably and uses the daily-average reading from wearables, giving you a truer number to coach from.

#### Consult

- **GIFs are back:** After the previous provider shut down, we moved to a new one, so GIFs are working in your messaging again.

- **Newest messages first:** New-message email notifications now lead with the most recent messages.

- **Readable, printable Daily Rx emails:** Daily Rx email content is now easier to read and print.

- **Message sync fix:** Client chat messages no longer appear out of order on web.

#### Design

- **Week visibility on long programs:** You always know which week you are working in when building long programs.

- **Hide Results toggle:** Collapse client results out of the way while you design.

- **Undo deletion in day mode:** Deleted a workout by accident? You can bring it back.

- **Day-notes where you need them:** Client day-notes now show on the dashboard feed and in workout modals.

- **Calendar focus follows your work:** Saving a workout highlights its day, including keyboard-shortcut saves.

#### Operate

- **Tablet-friendly layouts:** The Clients list, Lifestyle list, and Activation and Tasks panels now work well at tablet sizes.

- **Billing fixes:** Correct proration on the final invoice of fixed-length subscriptions, accurate trial end dates, restored receipt and billing emails, fixed tax-rate errors, clearer errors in the cancellation flow, and a direct link to the update-card modal.

- **Clean client transfers:** No more duplicate metrics when a client moves to a different organization.

- **Bug fixes and performance:** Dozens of bug fixes and performance improvements across the platform.

#### Client Experience

- **Every email now carries your brand:** All client- and coach-facing emails, including client invitations, use the new template and respect your custom theme.

- **Theming in onboarding:** New coaches are prompted to brand their client experience right from the activation panel.

- **Polished branded mobile experience:** A smooth splash transition, themed lifestyle and wearable screens, and a batch of visual fixes throughout the client app.

- **Better custom quotes:** Improved default loading quotes, a collapsed quotes list with Show more, and tighter validation when you save your own.

#### Switching to CoachRx

- **Custom exercise video migration:** Your custom exercise videos now come with you when you migrate with transition assistance.

- **Improved import:** Better data transfer when migrating to CoachRx.

- **Multi-coach organization support:** Smoother transitions for organizations with multiple coaches.

Stay tuned for more updates coming your way soon.
