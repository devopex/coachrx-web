We're excited to share workflow enhancements and collaboration features that make your coaching more efficient, transparent, and connected:

- **New Profile Hover :** Profile hover cards and status rings around avatars now give you instant insight into client status and needs. A quick view to see exactly clients need anywhere their profile is displayed.

- **Keyboard Shortcuts Update :** We've added several new keyboard shortcuts and dramatically improved their discoverability, readability, and usability site-wide. Navigate faster, work smarter, and keep your hands on the keyboard.

- **Business Suite:** Disputes Management : We've added Disputes functionality to your Business Suite, giving you the tools to handle billing issues and payment disputes directly within the platform. Manage these situations professionally without leaving your coaching workspace.

- **Expanded Health Data Insights :** We now generate several Health Data Insights specifically around respiratory rate, giving you deeper visibility into client recovery patterns and nervous system regulation. These insights complement your existing HRV data for a more complete recovery picture.

- **Enhanced Client List Compliance View :** Exercise and Lifestyle compliance on your clients list now displays all three compliance values with individual sorting capabilities. Get the exact view you need to identify clients who need check-ins or celebrate those crushing their programs.

- **Improved Exercise Library Navigation :** We've added sorting by exercise name on the exercise list along with several other usability enhancements to that screen. Finding and organizing your exercise library is now faster and more intuitive. Plus, we've improved performance when searching for exercises during workout programming, no more lag when you're in your design flow.
