We're excited to share powerful new AI capabilities coming soon, flexible business tools, and platform enhancements designed to help you coach more efficiently and grow your practice with confidence:

- **RxBot Updates (Closed Beta):** We're testing intelligent AI features to help you communicate faster and more effectively with clients. Get AI-generated message suggestions from the Client Insights and Needs Attention dashboard, receive smart feedback suggestions when commenting on workouts, and use RxBot to help craft and refine your messages and comments. These tools amplify your coaching voice while saving you valuable time.

- **Client Autonomy - Exercise Swapping:** Give clients the ability to make smart, real-time exercise adjustments without losing the intent of your programming. When equipment is busy, they're traveling, or training in a different environment, your clients can swap exercises while staying true to your design. CoachRx prioritizes swaps that match the same movement pattern, keeping your programming principles intact. You maintain complete control, decide which clients get access to this feature through your Client Autonomy settings. No need to reprogram entire sessions for minor adjustments, and workouts still get done when life happens.

- **Client Autonomy - Adding & Editing Workouts (Closed Beta):** Empower your clients to log additional training sessions or modify workouts directly from the mobile app when circumstances require it. Perfect for clients who add extra skill work, make up missed sessions, or need to adjust on the fly in ways you've approved. You maintain full control, turn this feature on or off for individual clients based on their needs and your coaching relationship. This keeps your clients moving forward with their training while giving you complete visibility and final say over their program.

- **Enterprise Plan:** A new flexible billing option that scales naturally with your business. Pay per-client and gain more control over your subscription structure. Perfect for growing coaching practices that need billing aligned with their business model.

- **Stripe Connect Standard Accounts:** We've upgraded payment processing for new accounts. Enjoy improved reliability and a more robust payment infrastructure for your business.

- **VO2 Max Trends Insights:** Track client cardiovascular fitness trends over time with new insights. Get deeper visibility into your clients' aerobic capacity development alongside your existing health data metrics.

- **Enhanced Client Onboarding:** New "Meet Your Coach" experience when clients accept their invitation creates a more personal, professional first impression and sets the tone for your coaching relationship.

- **Notification Navigation:** Navigate between notifications using arrow keys or buttons without closing the modal. Stay in flow while managing your communication queue.

- **GIF Picker (Mobile App):** Send GIFs in messages to add personality and warmth to your client communications. Sometimes a well-placed GIF says what words can't.

- **Transition Assistance Tools:** Improved resources to help coaches migrate smoothly from other platforms to CoachRx. We're making it even easier to make the switch without disrupting your business.

- **Faster Activity Feed:** Significant performance improvements for coaches managing larger client rosters. Your activity feed now loads and updates dramatically faster, especially for accounts with many clients.

- **Updated Keyboard Shortcuts:** Shortcuts now display in a clean slide-out panel with Mac/Windows-specific keys (⌘ vs Ctrl), making them more discoverable and easier to reference.

- **Better Weekly Check-in Display:** Cleaner, more readable weekly check-in cards on the client calendar give you faster insight into client responses and engagement.

- **Program Image Management:** Delete images from program sales pages to keep your storefront fresh and aligned with your current offerings.
