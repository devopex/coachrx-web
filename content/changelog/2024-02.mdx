What’s New:

- You can now save rest days with the same keyboard shortcut that you can save normal workouts

- We updated the fonts we use across the platform that should be much more readable and pleasing to the eye

- We are now calculating streak counts for clients. This feature is behind a feature flag and will be rolling out in batches to users over the coming days & weeks

- Exercise history now doesn't return loosely related Metrics.

- Add PPL with Aerobics and Strength to downloadable programs

- We now resize client avatars and serve them in an optimized format. We used to serve whatever image was uploaded by the client, regardless of how large they were

- Minor performance optimization around calculating client due dates

- Improved the styling of forms, inputs, and buttons across the platform

- Added a loading template to exercise history modal so you don't see a "No results" message while we are still fetching data from the server.
