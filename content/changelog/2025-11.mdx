We're excited to share that Lifestyle Rx Health Data & Trends is now officially live, plus we've added powerful new tools to streamline your coaching workflow:

- **Health Data & Trends - Now Live for All Users:** The comprehensive health data integration and lifestyle trends dashboard we announced in September is officially available to all CoachRx users. Connect your clients' wearables and health apps to automatically sync data from Apple Health, WHOOP, Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, and more. Every client profile now includes trend analysis across readiness scoring, sleep analytics, HRV data, and nutrition intelligence transforming how you monitor client progress and inform coaching decisions. Watch the demo video and read the feature article here. Voice Notes: Communicate with clients in a more personal, efficient way. Record and send voice messages directly within client conversations, perfect for providing detailed feedback, explaining complex concepts, or simply adding a human touch to your digital coaching. Voice notes make remote coaching feel more connected while saving you time on lengthy typed responses. Read more here. Flexible Intake Forms: Coaches can now choose which intake form gets sent to a client when adding them to your roster. Customize your onboarding experience based on client type, service level, or coaching focus ensuring you collect the right information from the start without requiring clients to fill out irrelevant forms. Consultation Templates Index Page: Access all your consultation templates in one organized location. We've added a dedicated templates page to the Index, making it easier to reference, update, and manage your consultation frameworks. Quickly find the right template for initial assessments, progress check-ins, or specialized consultations. Bug Fixes & Performance Improvements: We've made numerous under-the-hood enhancements to improve platform stability, speed, and overall user experience. Your CoachRx workspace now runs smoother and more reliably than ever.
