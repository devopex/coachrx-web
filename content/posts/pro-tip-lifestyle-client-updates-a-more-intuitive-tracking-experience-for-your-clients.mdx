The ultimate success of any coaching program depends on client compliance. Even the most brilliantly designed lifestyle prescriptions only work if your clients actually track them consistently. That's why we've completely reimagined the client-facing side of Lifestyle Rx to make tracking more intuitive, more specific, and more effective.

### **What It Is:**

The enhanced client mobile experience provides a more detailed and intuitive way for clients to track their lifestyle habits. Instead of simple yes/no responses, clients can now enter specific values that provide you with more meaningful data for your coaching decisions.

### **How to Use It:**

#### Step 1: Familiar Overall Layout

- Clients will still access the Lifestyle Rx section in their mobile app just as before
- They'll see "Today's Workout" and their "Lifestyle Prescription" sections,

#### Step 2: New Results Entry Options

- The big change comes in how clients enter their results
- Instead of binary "Yes/No" responses, clients can now enter specific values:

- For step goals: "Did you hit your 8,000-10,000 steps? Today I walked 9,537 steps"
- For hydration: "How many ounces of water did you drink? Today I drank 87 ounces"
![](/images/content/aeaa94509d-1.webp)![](/images/content/e5c5120929-2.webp)

#### **Step 3:** More Specific Feedback

- Clients can track exactly how close they came to their targets
- This provides a more nuanced view of compliance than simply "Yes, I did it" or "No, I didn't"
- Clients can see their progress toward goals more clearly

#### **Step 4:** Preparing Your Clients

- When the update goes live on April 14th, take a moment to notify your clients
- Ensure they update their mobile app to the latest version - this is critical for accessing the new features
- Send a reminder email or text encouraging them to check for updates in their app store
- Explain that they'll now be able to enter specific values rather than just yes/no responses
- Emphasize how this more detailed tracking will help you provide better coaching

### **Why It Matters:**

This enhanced client experience transforms how your clients interact with their lifestyle prescriptions:

- Better Accountability: Clients can't just say "yes" when they've only partially completed a task
- More Accurate Data: You'll know exactly how much water they drank, how many steps they took, etc.
- Trend Analysis: Track gradual improvements over time (e.g., steadily increasing step counts)
- Personalized Coaching: Adjust targets based on precise client feedback
- Client Motivation: Clients can see incremental progress, even when they don't fully hit targets

Remember,  that coaching happens during all 24 hours of the day—not just during training sessions. This enhanced mobile experience gives you unprecedented visibility into those critical hours where habits are formed and lifestyle choices are made.

The most successful coaches will take time to help their clients understand and embrace this new tracking method. A quick video walkthrough or personal demonstration during your next check-in can ensure clients are comfortable with the new interface.
