#### Maximizing Your Coaching Services

**The Perfect Setup Strategy:**

1. **Audit Your Current Clients**

   - List all services you currently provide
   - Note which clients receive which services
   - Identify opportunities for service expansion
2. **Optimize Your Dashboard**

   - Update settings for current clients
   - Set defaults for new clients
   - Create service packages for streamlined onboarding
3. **Leverage for Business Growth**

   - Create clear service boundaries
   - Package services effectively
   - Scale specific services based on demand

#### 🚀 Implementation Guide

**Quick Setup via Dashboard**

1. Navigate to "Needs Attention"
2. Click the three dots next to any client
3. Select their specific services

**Bulk Update via Profiles**

1. Head to client settings
2. Choose services for each client
3. Update in batches for efficiency

#### Pro Coach Tips for Service Success

**1. Quarterly Service Reviews**

Schedule regular check-ins to review and adjust services based on client progress and goals.

**2. Strategic Service Packaging**

Use service settings to create clear, profitable coaching packages:

- Basic: Single service focus
- Premium: Multi-service integration
- VIP: All services included

**3. Onboarding Excellence**

Incorporate service selection into your client onboarding process for clarity from day one.

#### Common Questions from Coaches

**Q: Can I change services after setting them up?** A: Yes! Services can be adjusted anytime as your client's needs evolve.

**Q: How does this affect existing client data?** A: Your historical data stays intact—you're just customizing what you see moving forward.

**Q: Can I offer different services to different clients?** A: Absolutely! Each client can have their own unique service configuration.
