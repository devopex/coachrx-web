### **The OPEX Philosophy in Action**

The enhanced Lifestyle Rx feature brings the OPEX "Life-Fuel-Person" framework to life with unprecedented clarity:

- **LIFE**: Track sleep, hydration, digestion, energy, movement, and stress with more detailed metrics than ever before.

### **From Simple Tracking to Comprehensive Behavior Change**

The core philosophy of Lifestyle Rx remains unchanged: to establish new behaviors and reinforce them until they become habits—not to create perpetual tracking tasks for your clients.

What has changed is the sophistication and flexibility of the tools at your disposal. This update transforms Lifestyle Rx from a simple habit-tracking tool to a comprehensive behavior change and nutrition management system that provides:

1. More detailed prescription options for truly personalized coaching
2. Better data collection from clients for deeper insights
3. Improved visualization of progress for more effective consultations
4. Streamlined workflow for efficient program design
5. Better integration with your overall coaching platform

### **How to Use the Enhanced Lifestyle Rx**

The intention of each Lifestyle Rx is not for your client to track it forever, but to help them establish a new behavior and reinforce it until it becomes a habit. With the enhanced features, you can be even more strategic:

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with 1-3 strategic daily habits for each client
2. **Track Strategically**: Use the new metric options to gather meaningful data, not just yes/no compliance
3. **Review Weekly**: Check compliance patterns and client feedback regularly
4. **Refine Monthly**: Use insights to adjust prescriptions during monthly consultations
5. **Visualize Progress**: Share compliance trends with clients to reinforce positive behaviors
