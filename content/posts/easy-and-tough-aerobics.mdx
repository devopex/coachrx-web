#### Easy & Tough Aerobics Program Breakdown

```
This 8-week aerobic program includes 3 easy and 1 tough aerobic sessions per week, which is the split we recommend when programming aerobic training for health and vitality clients. 

The program begins with a Ramp Test to establish baseline metrics, which can be used throughout the program to personalize intensity levels (objective and subjective). The program then ends with a Ramp Test to gauge easy and tough aerobic progress.

Who is Easy & Tough Aerobics For?

```
Training Age = Beginner - Advanced

Three key components should be incorporated into every training program: 
Resistance Training
Easy Aerobic Work

This 8-week program shows you how to implement easy and tough aerobics in one program, which is an ideal approach for any client who wants to maintain their physical health throughout life. Here’s a quick summary of the benefits of each:
```

Easy Aerobics

- ```
  More stamina for daily activities
  ```
- ```
  Enhanced endurance and recovery from resistance training and intense work
  ```
- ```
  Maintains and promotes the growth of healthy mitochondria, leading to better metabolic health and more efficient energy production
  ```
- ```
  Cognitive benefits from blood flow to the brain and increased brain-derived neurotrophic factor
  ```
- ```
  Benefits to the cardiovascular system
  ```
- ```
  Teaches patience, focus, and consistency 
  ```

Tough Aerobics

- ```
  Pushes the heart to pump more efficiently, increasing stroke volume and cardiac output, leading to enhanced oxygen delivery to the muscles
  ```
- ```
  Ultimately, greater aerobic capacity
  ```
- ```
  Improved vO2 max
  ```
- ```
  Challenges the body and mind
  ```

```
As you can see, a fitness program that includes both easy and tough aerobic training can provide profound health benefits and is recommended for anyone who wants to increase longevity and vitality.
