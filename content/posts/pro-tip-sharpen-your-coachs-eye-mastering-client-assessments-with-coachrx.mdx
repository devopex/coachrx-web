As a fitness coach, your ability to observe, analyze, and correct movement patterns is your most valuable asset. But even the most experienced coaches can miss crucial details without a systematic approach. That's where CoachRx's Assessment Feature comes in, serving as your digital third eye to enhance your coaching prowess.

#### The Power of the Coach's Eye

A keen "coach's eye" is the cornerstone of effective coaching. It allows you to:

- Quickly evaluate client movements
- Spot form deviations with precision
- Provide targeted corrections
- Reduce injury risk
- Boost overall performance

Developing this skill is essential, but having a structured, repeatable assessment process takes your coaching to the next level. It ensures you can tailor each client's program perfectly, eliminating guesswork and boosting your confidence.

#### Introducing CoachRx's Assessment Feature

Our Assessment Feature is designed to enhance your observational abilities, enabling you to analyze and correct movement patterns with unprecedented precision. Here's what it offers:

#### What It Does:

- Provides a structured framework for movement observation
- Focuses on fundamental patterns: squats, hinges, pushes, pulls
- Offers detailed movement breakdowns
- Integrates assessment priorities directly into training plans

#### Why It Matters:

1. **Structured Observation:** Consistently evaluate and refine client movements
2. **Detailed Analysis:** Identify both minor and major issues with ease
3. **Client-Specific Insights:** Create personalized corrective strategies
4. **Enhanced Feedback:** Improve client communication and engagement
5. **Continuous Improvement:** Refine your observational skills over time
6. **Integrated Priorities:** Automatically generate customized training plans
7. **Coaching Confidence:** Eliminate second-guessing with a repeatable process

#### How to Leverage the Assessment Feature

Ready to take your coaching to the next level? Here's how to use CoachRx's Assessment Feature:

1. **Access the Tools:**

   - Log into CoachRx
   - Navigate to the Assessment section
   - Start with OPEX Body, OPEX Move, and OPEX Work
2. **OPEX Body:**

   - Record body composition details like weight and body fat percentage
   - Add personalized notes on client's physique
3. **OPEX Move:**

   - Assign pass/fail status for each movement assessment
   - For failures, select faults and potential causes
   - Let CoachRx generate integrated priorities for training plans
4. **OPEX Work:**

   - Conduct the 10-minute Air Bike test
   - Get normalized scores based on client weight
