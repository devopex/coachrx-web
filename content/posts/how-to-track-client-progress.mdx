### **How to Track Client Progress**

#### **Comprehensive Assessment Tools**

```
"If you're not assessing, you're guessing." This is a core principle in the world of coaching, and for good reason. Understanding your clients' starting points is essential for designing effective fitness programs. CoachRx offers a robust assessment system that allows you to evaluate various facets of your clients' fitness journey. 

CoachRx has integrated the OPEX assessment into the CoachRx platform, or you can choose to use your own assessment systems. The OPEX assessment is divided into three main components:

OPEX Body: Analyzing your client's height, weight, lean body mass, and fat mass.
OPEX Move: Assessing motor control across six fundamental movement patterns: squat, bend, push, pull, lunge, and core.
OPEX Work: Conducting basic energy system testing, including a 10-minute Airbike test for work capacity assessment.

With CoachRx, inputting and storing assessment data is a breeze, ensuring easy access for both you and your clients. This data forms the foundation upon which you can build tailored fitness plans and track client progress against ongoing.
```

#### **Strength and Energy Systems Metrics**

```
Metrics are a game-changer in tracking client progress. These collections of exercises serve as benchmarks to monitor your clients' fitness levels and their readiness to advance to the next stage. CoachRx offers both energy system and resistance metrics, with options to create custom metrics tailored to your clients' specific needs.

Energy system metrics are categorized into beginner, intermediate, and advanced tests, providing a clear path for clients to graduate to higher levels. On the other hand, resistance metrics cover a comprehensive range of strength and endurance assessments for each movement pattern.
```

#### **Structural Balance Insights**

```
CoachRx goes a step further by offering structural balance insights based on your clients' resistance metrics scores. These insights are invaluable in identifying imbalances, both left to right and upper to lower body, as well as exercise-specific discrepancies. For example, if a client's deadlift is significantly higher or lower than their back squat, CoachRx will flag this, helping you prioritize the appropriate movement pattern in your programming.

Structural balance is essential for injury prevention and optimal performance, making it a crucial aspect of progress tracking.
```

#### **Data Visualization**

```
Numbers alone can be overwhelming. CoachRx simplifies the process by providing clear and intuitive visual representations of your clients' progress each time they track a metric. Through easy-to-read charts and graphs, you and your clients can track improvements over time. This visual feedback not only motivates clients but also helps them see the tangible results of their hard work.

CoachRx extends its accessibility through a mobile app that allows your clients to view their progress, exercise history, metrics history, and workout plans on the go. This keeps clients engaged and motivated, enhancing their commitment to long-term fitness.
```
