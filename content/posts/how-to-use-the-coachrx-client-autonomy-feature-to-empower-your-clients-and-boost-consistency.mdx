The first of many new **client autonomy features** inside CoachRx lets you give more decision making power to your your clients when you feel they are ready. With coach-enabled settings, you decide who can manage their own workout schedule, letting clients enjoy more flexibility and stay consistent, even when life gets busy. More client autonomy features are in the works, be sure to keep an eye on the [change log](/change-log).

#### Why Client Autonomy Is a Win for Coaching

Giving your clients the ability to move their workouts gives them a real say in their training implamentation. When clients can shift their sessions as needed, they feel responsible for their progress. That’s not just helpful, it leads to lasting change.

**Benefits include:**

- Flexibility to fit workouts around real-life events
- Consistent training even during busy weeks
- A sense of ownership over their fitness journey

#### What the New Client Autonomy Feature Does

This *first release* of client autonomy gives your clients the ability to move their assigned workouts within the app. Clients can now easily drag a session to a day that fits, without losing touch with their program, this will be reflected in your view of the client calendar also. There’s more *ongoing development* planned, so expect this feature to grow.

#### How Coaches Control Client Autonomy

You stay in the driver’s seat with client autonomy. The feature doesn’t turn on by default. You choose when a client is ready perhaps after some education and a chat about expectations. Turn it on only for those who’re truly prepared for that step.

**Always set clear expectations before enabling autonomy for clients.**

#### How to Enable Client Autonomy in CoachRx

Making this feature work for your clients is simple. Here’s how you activate client autonomy, step by step:

1. Log into your CoachRx platform.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Click on **Client Settings**.
4. Scroll down until you see **Client Autonomy**.
5. Select **Configure**.
6. Choose the clients you want to enable this for.
7. Click **Save Settings**.

**Enable autonomy when a client demonstrates responsibility and you’ve discussed how it works.**

#### When Should You Let Clients Move Their Workouts?

Autonomy isn’t for everyone, or for every situation.

Common times to enable it:

- Clients with irregular or hectic schedules
- More experienced clients comfortable with flexible training

***Tip:*** *Always have a conversation about expectations and make sure your client understands how to use this option responsibly.*

#### How Clients Move Workouts in the Mobile App

From the client’s viewpoint, it’s easy. When they open the app, they land on their daily RX home screen. Here, they see both their workout plan and lifestyle goals. The new **move** icon lets them shift a workout by opening a calendar view. They pick a new date, tap **move**, and the workout jumps to that day. Want to move it back? Repeat the process.

#### What Changes on the Coach Calendar?

When a client moves a workout, their coach’s calendar shows the change right away. Any session that was on Monday but gets moved to Tuesday will appear correctly on the coach’s schedule.

**Rest easy, coach oversight remains intact, and all scheduling updates are instant.**

#### Why Client Autonomy Helps Coaches

This feature adds more than client convenience. It also makes your life easier. Here’s how:

- Less back-and-forth rescheduling
- Fewer missed sessions and more client accountability
- Better insights into real-life training habits

#### Why Client Autonomy Helps Clients

Giving clients control over their schedule is empowering. They don’t have to message you for every small change, and it gives them *personal responsibility for their progress*. The freedom boosts both their consistency and motivation.

#### Tips for Rolling Out Autonomy

Talking to your clients before turning on this feature smooths the transition.

**Coach’s Tips:**

- Explain how and when to reschedule workouts
- Set boundaries on moving sessions (e.g., not skipping key weeks)
- Remind clients that flexible doesn’t mean skipping

#### What to Watch For

This feature is still growing, so keep an eye out for future updates. Without clear guidelines, some clients might get off track, so always remind them of your training approach and expectations.

#### Consistency and Flexibility Go Hand in Hand

**Consistency** and **flexibility** don’t have to compete. When clients have control, they’re more likely to stay on track without feeling boxed in by a rigid plan. That balance drives steady, lasting results.

#### Walkthrough: Client Rescheduling in Action

Here’s how it works for a client:

- Open the mobile app and see the daily RX screen.
- Tap the move icon next to a workout.
- Pick the new date (say, from Monday to Tuesday) in the calendar popup.
- Confirm, and the workout jumps to the new day.
- Want to undo it? Just repeat these steps.

It’s that fast. This all syncs right back to the coach’s calendar so you never lose track.
