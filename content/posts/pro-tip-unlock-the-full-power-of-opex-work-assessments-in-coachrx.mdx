Ever notice how two clients can get the same score on a test but require completely different programming approaches? That's because raw numbers only tell part of the story. The real coaching magic happens when you can interpret those metrics and turn them into personalized programming decisions.

### **What It Is:**

The OPEX Work assessment tracking in CoachRx is a comprehensive system for administering, analyzing, and tracking the 10-minute Assault Bike test. Unlike basic testing that only provides raw scores, this feature automatically calculates body weight-adjusted values using the OPEX formula, tracks progress over time with visual graphs, and helps you identify individual client limiters.

### **How to Use It:**

#### Step 1: Administer and Record the Assessment

- Navigate to your client's profile and select "Assessment" from the sidebar
- Enter their current body weight in the OPEX Body section
- Record their raw score (calories achieved in 10 minutes) in the OPEX Work section
- Note how the system automatically calculates their body weight-adjusted score
![](/images/content/2864c00905-1.webp)

#### Step 2: Analyze Beyond the Numbers

- Ask follow-up questions to identify true limiters:

- “How did you feel during this test?"
- "What was the limiting factor for you?"
- "How did your pacing strategy work?"

- Observe their performance pattern (steady pace vs. fast start/crash)
- Consider their training age and experience with the equipment

#### Step 3: Track Progress Visually

- View the client's metrics graph under "Energy System Beginner"
- Compare results across different testing periods
- Correlate changes with your programming phases
- Use the visual representation to explain progress to your client
![](/images/content/a3381e545d-2.webp)

#### Step 4: Connect Insights to Programming

- For novice clients with low scores: Focus on equipment familiarity and basic aerobic development
- For clients who start fast and crash: Address pacing strategies and sustainable output
- For clients with specific limiters: Target programming to address those weaknesses
- Reassess strategically (not too frequently) to allow adaptations to occur

### **Why It Matters:**

As James FitzGerald often says, "Assessment offers you the truth." But that truth is only valuable if you can interpret it correctly and connect it to personalized programming decisions.

When you master this approach to assessment, you transform from a coach who simply collects data to one who delivers truly individualized programming based on objective insights. This creates several powerful outcomes:

- Better Results: Programs that address clients' actual limitations produce faster, more sustainable progress
- Increased Value Perception: Clients see that you're not giving them random workouts, but tailored solutions
- Higher Retention\*: When clients understand how your decisions connect to their assessment data, their buy-in and compliance significantly improve
- Professional Differentiation: In a crowded market, this data-driven approach sets you apart as a true professional

Remember, your clients aren't paying for random training—they're paying for your expertise in recognizing and addressing their individual needs. The OPEX Work assessment gives you the tools to deliver exactly that.
