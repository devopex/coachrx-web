#### **Understanding the Most Lucrative Client Segments**

Individuals aged 45 to 54 make up the largest segment at 28.0%, closely followed by those aged 35 to 44 at 26.2%. According to the industry association IDEA and the World Health Organization, the baby boomer generation alone holds over 50.0% of the nation’s discretionary income and prioritizes spending on health above all else.

#### **Why This Audience Expects High-Quality Professional Service**

1. **Experience and Expectation:** These clients have high expectations for service quality. They value professionalism, consistency, and results.
2. **Health Focus:** They are highly motivated to invest in their health, seeking long-term wellness and prevention.
3. **Discretionary Income:** With substantial disposable income, they are willing to pay for premium services that promise better outcomes and personal attention.
4. **Tech Savvy:** Many are comfortable using technology and expect seamless interaction and engagement through digital platforms.
