One of the most powerful aspects of premium coaching is personalization. Every client has unique needs, goals, and lifestyle factors that influence their success. With the enhanced Lifestyle Rx feature, you now have unprecedented flexibility to create custom lifestyle types that collect exactly the data you need.

### **What It Is:**

The updated Lifestyle Rx system allows you to create custom lifestyle categories with various tracking options that go far beyond simple yes/no completion. You can now specify exactly how you want clients to track each habit, from quality scores to specific measurements.

### **How to Use It:**

#### Step 1: Navigate to the Index Section

- Go to the Index tab in your CoachRx dashboard
- Select "Lifestyle Rx" from the menu options
- Click "Add Lifestyle Rx" to create a new custom type
![](/images/content/33aa7e47af-1.webp)

#### Step 2: Categorize Your Custom Type

- Select which category your new lifestyle type belongs to (Sleep, Energy, Hydration, etc.)
- Provide a clear description for the type
- Add specific instructions for your clients
![](/images/content/d17846ea1b-2.webp)

#### **Step 3:** Choose Your Tracking Method

- This is where the magic happens! Select from five different tracking options:

- Scale (1-5): Perfect for subjective quality ratings (sleep quality, energy levels, stress)
- Count: Ideal for tracking specific quantities (ounces of water, grams of protein, servings of vegetables)
- Time/Duration: Great for tracking how long clients spend on certain activities
- Yes/No: Simple completion tracking for straightforward habits
- Specific Time: Track when clients complete certain activities

#### **Step 4:** Set Specific Parameters

- For count-based tracking, specify the unit of measurement (ounces, grams, servings, etc.)
- Set target values for clients to aim for
- Configure automatic reminders to prompt client entries

#### **Step 5:** Preview the Client Experience

- Use the new "Client Preview" feature to see exactly what your client will see in their mobile app

- Ensure the tracking method makes sense from the client's perspective

### **Why It Matters:**

This enhanced customization transforms how you gather and use client data:

- More Meaningful Data: Move beyond binary yes/no tracking to capture the nuance of client behaviors
- Better Client Understanding: Collect data that shows not just compliance but quality and quantity
- Personalized Coaching: Create tracking methods tailored to each client's specific needs and goals
- Improved Accountability: Set specific targets that clients can measure against
- Enhanced Communication: Use precise data points to drive more meaningful consultation discussions

Remember, the OPEX "Life-Fuel-Person" framework is all about personalization. With these new custom lifestyle types, you can create truly individualized tracking systems that reflect the unique needs of each client. This level of personalization is what separates premium coaching from generic programs..
