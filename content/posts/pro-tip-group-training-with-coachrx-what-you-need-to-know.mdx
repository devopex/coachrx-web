#### Why This Matters

Group training is a game-changer for scaling your business, but it comes with its own set of challenges. How do you keep everyone on track, engaged, and feeling personally attended to? That's where CoachRx steps in to make your life easier and your training more impactful.

### CoachRx's Secret Weapons for Group Training Success

#### 1. Programs

**What it does:** CoachRx allows you to create either standard or live programs for individuals or groups. A standard program isn’t tied to specific calendar dates—it's designed to run for a set number of days and ends when completed. Alternatively, a live program is tied to calendar dates, allowing you to update workouts in real-time and push them to all clients in the program. This ongoing design is perfect for mass-sending the same program to multiple clients without a defined end date.

**Recommended For Groups:** When managing group training, efficiency is key, which is why we recommend using the live program feature, allowing you to deliver workouts in real-time to all participants. This not only ensures consistency across the group but also keeps everyone on the same page, literally!

**Key Benefits:**

- Flexibility to adjust programs on the fly
- Ensures all participants are on the same page
- Perfect for ongoing programs without a defined end date

**Pro Tip:** To maximize the impact of your group sessions, combine the live program feature with mass messaging. This allows you to communicate crucial updates, motivational notes, or quick adjustments to everyone in the group at once. This combination will keep your group engaged and moving toward their fitness goals together

#### 2. Mass Messaging

**What it does:** Enables you to communicate with your entire group at once.

**Key Benefits:**

- Share updates, motivation, and adjustments efficiently
- Boost engagement and accountability
- Save time on individual communications

Your Step-by-Step Guide to Group Training Mastery

1. **Create a Live Program:**

   - Navigate to the Programs tab
   - Click "Add Program"
   - Select "Live Program"
   - Design your program to suit your group's needs
2. **Assign the Program:**

   - Assign your live program to all group fitness clients
3. **Tag Your Clients:**

   - Create and assign corresponding tags to all group fitness clients for easy management
4. **Utilize Mass Messaging:**

   - Use this feature for check-ins, encouragement, and addressing group questions

### Pro Tips for Group Training Excellence

1. **Personalize Within the Group:** Use individual comments alongside mass messages to maintain a personal touch.
2. **Progressive Overload:** Utilize the live program feature to gradually increase intensity or complexity over time.
3. **Theme Your Programs:** Create engaging themes or challenges to keep the group motivated.
4. **Feedback Loop:** Regularly ask for group feedback to continuously improve your program.
5. **Upsell Opportunity:** Use group success stories to showcase the benefits of your 1:1 coaching services.

### Your 7-Day Group Training Launch Plan

1. **Day 1-2:** Design your live program
2. **Day 3:** Assign program and tags to your group clients
3. **Day 4:** Craft a welcome mass message
4. **Day 5:** Run a test day and gather initial feedback
5. **Day 6:** Make any necessary adjustments
6. **Day 7:** Full launch with a motivational kick-off mass message!
