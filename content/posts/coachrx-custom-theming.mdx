### Your Brand Shouldn't Stop at the Storefront

**Custom Theming brings your colors, logo, typography, and voice into the app your clients open every day.**

It is a new way to brand the CoachRx client experience with your own colors, typography, logo, and words, so the app a client opens feels like their coach's, not a generic platform.

**It is live now, and it is included on every plan.**

![](/images/content/03e0aeb245-opex-july26-coachrx-feature-release-custom-theming-1x1-6.webp)

### What Custom Theming does

Everything is configured from a dedicated Theme page in Settings, with a live preview so you can see each change before you commit to it. When you are ready, you save. If you want to start over, you revert to defaults. No design help required.

Inside the Theme editor you can:

- Set your primary brand color from a curated set of presets or by entering your own hex value, with an optional gradient treatment derived from that color.
- Choose from curated typography options selected to stay legible across every screen of the app.
- Upload your coach logo and an optional custom splash image, so the client app opens into a branded splash screen instead of a generic one.
- Add a custom quote set that surfaces on the high-traffic screens your clients see most.

### Where your clients experience it

Once your theme is published, your branding follows the client into the daily training experience. Your color scheme applies to key elements across Home, Program, and Messages. The Home header features your avatar. The app opens to a splash screen that is unmistakably yours.

The result is a client experience that reinforces who they hired, from the first screen onward, every time they train.

#### **Why it matters for your practice**

For coaches building a serious business, brand consistency is not a cosmetic detail. It is part of what justifies premium pricing. A polished, consistent experience signals a professional practice, and it carries your identity past the sales page and into the place your clients actually spend their time.

Your brand no longer stops at the storefront. It reaches all the way into the daily work of coaching, which is exactly where trust is built and retention is earned.

#### **Why it matters for your clients**

People do not buy coaching. They buy the coach. Custom Theming makes that visible. Your clients open an app that looks and feels like you, from your colors and logo to your voice on the screens they see most. It reinforces who they chose to work with, not what software that coach happens to use.

That reminder matters. Every session becomes a small, consistent signal of who is in their corner.
