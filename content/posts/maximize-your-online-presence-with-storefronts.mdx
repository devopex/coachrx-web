### Maximize Your Online Presence with Storefronts

With the CoachRx Storefront feature, take your coaching business to the next level by creating an attractive and effective online marketplace. This powerful tool allows you to market and sell your coaching services, programs, and products seamlessly. Here’s how you can maximize your online presence using the CoachRx Storefront:

1. Connect to Stripe: First, make sure your CoachRx account is connected to Stripe, as this will enable you to handle online transactions efficiently.
2. Create Your Storefront: Access the 'Storefront' option in the Business Suite and click 'Create' to start crafting your storefront page. You have the choice between creating an Organization Storefront or a Coach Storefront, depending on your business needs.

   - Organization Storefront: Ideal for showcasing your business as a whole, including all coaches, programs, services, FAQs, client testimonials, and contact information.
   - Coach Storefront: Perfect for individual coaches within an organization to highlight their unique coaching style, programs, and services.
3. Customize Your Page: Add compelling copy, engaging images, and videos to your storefront to make it appealing. This is your chance to showcase your brand’s personality and what makes your coaching services unique.
4. Set Up Products: Before adding offerings to your storefront, set up your products and services in CoachRx. These could include coaching sessions, digital products, training programs, and more.
5. Add Products to Storefront: Once your products are ready, add them to your storefront. This allows clients and potential clients to browse and purchase your offerings directly from the page.
6. Go Live: After setting up your storefront, set the page to 'Live' and save your changes. You now have a URL for your storefront which can be shared on social media, embedded on your website, or sent directly to potential clients.
7. Client Account Creation: When a client makes a purchase through your storefront, a CoachRx client account is automatically created for them, making the onboarding process smooth and professional.
