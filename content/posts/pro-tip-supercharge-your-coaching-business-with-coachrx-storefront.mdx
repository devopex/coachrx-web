#### Why CoachRx Storefront is a Game-Changer

1. **Expand Your Reach**: With CoachRx Storefront, you're no longer limited by geography. Attract clients from around the globe with a professional, accessible online presence.

1. **Streamline Sales**: Say goodbye to complicated payment processes. CoachRx Storefront integrates seamlessly with Stripe, ensuring smooth and secure transactions.
2. **Boost Professionalism**: First impressions matter. CoachRx Storefront automatically creates client accounts, providing a seamless and professional onboarding experience.
3. **Showcase Your Brand**: Your coaching style is unique—shouldn't your online presence reflect that? Customize your storefront to highlight what makes your coaching special.
4. **Flexibility**: Whether you're a solo coach or part of a larger organization, CoachRx Storefront has you covered with options for both individual and organization-wide storefronts.

#### Launching Your Storefront: A Step-by-Step Guide

Ready to get started? Here's how to launch your CoachRx Storefront in six easy steps:

**1. Connect to Stripe**

Ensure your CoachRx account is linked to Stripe for seamless, secure online transactions.

**2. Create Your Storefront**

Navigate to the 'Storefront' option in the Business Suite and click 'Create'.

**3. Choose Your Style**

Decide between:

- **Organization Storefront**: Showcase your entire business, including all coaches, programs, services, FAQs, client testimonials, and contact information.
- **Coach Storefront**: Highlight an individual coach's unique style, programs, and services within the organization.

**4. Customize Your Page**

This is where you bring your brand to life! Add compelling copy, engaging images, and videos that showcase your personality and coaching philosophy.

**5. Set Up Products**

Define your offerings in CoachRx. This could include coaching sessions, digital products, training programs, and more.

**6. Go Live**

Set your page to 'Live', save your changes, and start sharing your storefront URL. Embed it on your website, share it on social media, or send it directly to potential clients.
