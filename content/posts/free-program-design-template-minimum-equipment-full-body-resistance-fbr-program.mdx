#### **Minimum Equipment Full Body Resistance (FBR) Program - 4 Weeks**

We're thrilled to announce the latest addition to the CoachRx program library: **Minimum Equipment Full Body Resistance (FBR) Program - 4 Weeks**. This program is a game-changer for coaches working with clients who have limited access to equipment but still want to achieve comprehensive, full-body workouts.

Let's dive into why this program matters and how it can transform your coaching approach for clients with minimal equipment.

#### **Why Minimum Equipment FBR Matters**

**Versatility in Limited Settings:** The program focuses on utilizing dumbbells, kettlebells, and bands, ensuring that clients can get a full-body workout regardless of their equipment limitations.

**Comprehensive Coverage:** Despite the minimal equipment, the program targets all major resistance patterns, providing a well-rounded approach to strength training.

**Consistent Progress:** The 3x per week, 4-week structure allows for steady progression and adaptation, perfect for clients looking to maintain or build strength with limited resources.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**Strategic Exercise Selection:** Each workout includes a squat, pull, bend, push, and core exercise, ensuring all major movement patterns are covered in every session.

**Balanced Workout Structure:** The program alternates the order of exercises between sessions, allowing for balanced fatigue management and recovery.

**Progressive Overload:** While the specific progression isn't detailed, the 4-week structure allows for incremental increases in weight, reps, or time under tension to ensure continuous improvement.

#### **Weekly Schedule Breakdown**

1. **Monday:** Squat, Pull, Bend, Push, Core

- Focus: Full-body strength with emphasis on lower body and posterior chain

2. **Wednesday:** Bend, Push, Lunge, Pull, Core

- Focus: Full-body strength with variation in lower body movement (lunge) and order of exercises

3. **Friday:** Squat, Pull, Bend, Push, Core

- Focus: Repeats Monday's structure for consistent practice and progression

This carefully planned schedule ensures that clients hit all major movement patterns multiple times per week, maximizing strength and muscle development with minimal equipment.
