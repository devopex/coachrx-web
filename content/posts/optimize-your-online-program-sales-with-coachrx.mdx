#### **Pro Tip: Optimize Your Online Program Sales with CoachRx**

If you’re planning to sell your program to a large audience, it’s crucial to keep your personal coaching roster separate from your program’s clients. This way, you can manage and organize your client base more effectively.

Think of this as having a dedicated manager for a specific department in a business. Just as a manager focuses on one department, separating your program’s clients from your personal coaching roster allows you to focus on different client groups without overlap.

**Here’s how to do it:**

To keep your program clients separate from your personal coaching roster, follow this straightforward approach:

1. **Create a New "Coach":** Start by creating a new "Coach" using your program’s name. This step will help distinguish your program clients from your personal clients.
2. **Update the Coach Field:** Head to the products section in CoachRx and update the “Coach” field to your program. This way, each client that purchases will be under your program’s roster instead of your personal roster.
