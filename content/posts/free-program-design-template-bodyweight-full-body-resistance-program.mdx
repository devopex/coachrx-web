#### **No Gym, No Problem: Introducing the Bodyweight Full Body Resistance Program****- 4 Weeks**

We're excited to announce the latest addition to the CoachRx program library: **Bodyweight Full Body Resistance (FBR) Program - 4 Weeks**. This program is a game-changer for coaches working with clients who are on-the-go, traveling, or simply prefer working out at home without specialized equipment.

Let's explore why this program is a must-have in your coaching toolkit and how it can keep your clients progressing no matter where they are.

#### **Why Bodyweight FBR Matters**

**Ultimate Flexibility:** Designed for clients without access to equipment, this program ensures consistent training during summer travel or any situation where gym access is limited.

**Everyday Object Integration:** By incorporating common household items, the program adds resistance and variety to bodyweight exercises, enhancing effectiveness.

**Full-Body Coverage:** Despite the lack of traditional equipment, the program targets all major resistance patterns, providing a comprehensive strength training approach.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**Strategic Exercise Selection:** Each workout includes a squat, pull, bend, push, and core exercise, ensuring all major movement patterns are covered in every session.

**Balanced Workout Structure:** The program alternates the order of exercises between sessions, allowing for balanced fatigue management and recovery.

**Progressive Overload:** The 4-week structure allows for incremental increases in reps, time under tension, or exercise difficulty to ensure continuous improvement.

#### **Weekly Schedule Breakdown**

1. **Monday:** Squat, Pull, Bend, Push, Core

- Focus: Full-body strength with emphasis on lower body and posterior chain

2. **Wednesday:** Bend, Push, Lunge, Pull, Core

- Focus: Full-body strength with variation in lower body movement (lunge) and order of exercises

3. **Friday:** Squat, Pull, Bend, Push, Core

- Focus: Repeats Monday's structure for consistent practice and progression

This thoughtfully planned schedule ensures that clients hit all major movement patterns multiple times per week, maximizing strength and muscle development using only bodyweight and everyday objects.
