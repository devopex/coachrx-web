#### **Transform Client Engagement with CoachRx's Powerful Touchpoints Feature**

In today’s fitness industry, where training programs are abundant and fitness advice is everywhere, what sets successful coaches apart isn't just their knowledge—it's their ability to build strong, meaningful relationships with their clients.

At CoachRx, we dive deep into what truly drives client success and retention. Here’s an insight we’ve uncovered across coaches inside CoachRx: the north star metric for coaching is compliance. And one of the most powerful tools for driving compliance is Touchpoints.

### **The Power of Touchpoints**

Research and our own data show that consistent, meaningful interactions between coaches and clients are crucial. It’s not just about creating the perfect program; it’s about being there, engaging, and supporting clients in their journey. Here’s what we’ve found:

- **Less than 1 touchpoint per week: 50% compliance**
- **1 touchpoint per week: 58% compliance**
- **More than 2 touchpoints per week: 75% compliance!!!**

In CoachRx, Touchpoints are the key to building and maintaining these crucial relationships. They are the measure of your interactions—whether you’re commenting on a workout, sending a direct message, conducting a consult, or checking in on lifestyle prescriptions.

#### **How Touchpoints Work**

**Set Your Goal**

CoachRx automatically sets your touchpoint goal at 1 per week, but you can adjust this in your organization settings to better suit your coaching style and client needs.

**Track Your Progress**

Your dashboard displays your touchpoint progress, and you can also view touchpoints per client. This visibility helps you stay on top of your interactions and ensures you’re providing the consistent engagement your clients need.

**Make It Meaningful**

Aim to make each touchpoint impactful. Avoid quick, impersonal messages. Instead, provide valuable feedback, celebrate milestones, and offer genuine support. This personal touch helps build trust and keeps clients motivated.

#### **The Impact of Meaningful Interactions**

Building strong client relationships through regular, meaningful interactions leads to higher compliance and better results. It’s the consistent, personal touchpoints that help clients feel supported and motivated.
