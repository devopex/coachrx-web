*We sat down with Dr. Austin Schoen, sports chiropractor, owner of* [*Warrior Sports Wellness*](https://www.instagram.com/warriorsportswellness/)*, and co-owner of the education and training company* [*Building a Fighter*](https://www.instagram.com/buildingafighter/)*, to learn how he’s using technology to level up his businesses. Working primarily with football and MMA athletes, both in-person and remotely, he combines sports medicine with performance training into one succinct package.*

```
My name is Austin Schoen and I am a sports chiropractor and strength coach in Scottsdale, Arizona. I combine sports med and performance training into one succinct package for my athletes. At my clinic we have a primary focus on working with MMA and Football athletes, but also can see a wide variety of patients from all walks of life. Outside of the in person clinic, I am also the Co-Owner of an education/training company called Building A Fighter that aims to elevate the level of care MMA athletes are receiving. We use online programming to assist combat sports athletes worldwide as well as have a series of courses that will be rolled out in the next calendar year to help other performance professionals understand how to work with combat athletics in a better way.
```

#### WHY IS IT IMPORTANT FOR S&C COACHES TO EMBRACE TECHNOLOGY?

```
Technology leads to efficiency which leads to helping more clients. By embracing modern tech and moving towards implementing it in your routine you open up a world of possibilities for working with clients even when you're not there. One of the biggest changes in how I operate as a sports chiro/ strength coach is the implementation of a programming software to manage my athletes compliance and understanding. Whether it's custom rehab plans or their performance programs, the use of CoachRx has allowed me to get more out of my athletes and coach athletes all over the world. On top of the programing software, the implementation of heart rate training, VBT devices, and specific blood flow restriction cuffs have helped change the way I program and coach my athletes on the floor.
```
