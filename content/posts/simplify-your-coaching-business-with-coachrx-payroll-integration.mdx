As a coach, managing your business efficiently is just as crucial as delivering top-notch training. That’s why we’ve integrated a powerful payroll feature within CoachRx, in collaboration with Stripe, to streamline your business operations and eliminate the need for multiple software solutions.

**Business Suite Pro Tip: How To Set Up & Manage Payroll**

For coaches managing a team, the Business Suite's payroll feature simplifies financial management significantly. Here’s how you can benefit:

1. Connect to Stripe: Essential for activating the payroll capabilities.
2. Set Up Commissions: Ensure accurate payroll calculations by assigning appropriate commission structures to your products, clients, and coaches.
3. Run Payroll Seamlessly: Select the payroll period, review items, make necessary adjustments, and execute payroll—all within CoachRx.

#### **Efficient Payroll Management:**

1. With CoachRx Payroll, you can seamlessly run payroll directly through CoachRx, saving time and reducing administrative hassle. While, there is more than one way to use the payroll feature and know that each coach will find a flow that works best for them, we recommend the following workflow:

   - Choose Date Range: Select the specific period for payroll processing.
   - Review and Adjust: Check details and add any additional compensation if required.
   - Export Data: Optional CSV export for detailed payment reports.
   - Fulfill Payroll: Complete the payroll process with just a few clicks.
   - Review Metrics: Keep track of payroll metrics to ensure everything runs smoothly.
