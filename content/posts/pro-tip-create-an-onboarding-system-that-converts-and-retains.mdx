First impressions are everything in coaching. The difference between a one-month client and a one-year client often comes down to how professional and organized your onboarding process feels. Today, I want to share how to create an onboarding system that sets the foundation for long-term client relationships.

### **What It Is:**

A comprehensive digital onboarding system in CoachRx that combines legal documentation, client education, and payment processing into one streamlined, professional experience.

### **How to Use It:**

**Set Up Legal Documents**

- Create contract templates
- Prepare liability waivers
- Configure digital signing process
- Store signed documents securely
- Send automated copies to clients
![](/images/content/b79775afe6-1.webp)

**Build Client Education Packets**

- Create program design expectations
- Define communication guidelines
- Outline client responsibilities
- Include platform usage instructions
- Add coaching policies
- Pro tip: Upload as PDF for mobile app access
![](/images/content/fc6761674f-2.webp)

**Configure Payment Systems**

- Set up Stripe integration
- Create product offerings
- Configure subscription plans
- Automate billing processes
- Track payments efficiently
![](/images/content/1e8bd5cc8e-3.webp)

**Customize Organization Details**

- Add company logo
- Update contact information
- Brand all client-facing documents
- Ensure professional presentation
![](/images/content/cf61bc0dca-4.webp)

### **Why It Matters:**

#### This system transforms your coaching by:

- Creating professional first impressions
- Preventing misunderstandings
- Securing necessary legal protection
- Streamlining payment collection
- Saving administrative time
- Setting clear expectations
- Building client confidence
- Reducing early dropoffs
