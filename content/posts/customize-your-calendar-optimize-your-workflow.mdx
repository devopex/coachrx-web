### Customize Your Calendar, Optimize Your Workflow

Your work environment, including the digital tools you use, plays a significant role in your productivity and workflow. CoachRx recognizes this and offers customizable calendar features to enhance your program design process. Today, we're exploring how you can tailor the CoachRx calendar to fit your unique workflow, making your coaching more efficient and effective.

**Program Design Pro Tip: Tailoring Your Calendar in CoachRx**

CoachRx is more than just a coaching platform; it’s a tool designed to adapt to your personal workflow. Here’s how you can customize your calendar in CoachRx for optimal efficiency:

- **Date Ranges Flexibility:** Choose to design with a view of a single day, a week, or even an entire month. This flexibility allows you to plan programs according to your preference and client needs.
- **Exercise or Lifestyle Focus:** Switch between viewing the exercise calendar and the lifestyle calendar. This feature is perfect for tailoring your design focus to specific aspects of client programming.
- **Daily Tags Option:** Opt to show or hide daily tags from your short-term plan. This feature helps save screen space and allows you to view these tags only when necessary.
- **Truncate Text for Clarity:** Expand or shorten the text to clean up your screen during the design process. A cleaner screen can lead to a clearer mind and a more focused workflow.

**CoachRx: Adapting to Your Unique Coaching Style**

These customizable features ensure that CoachRx adapts to your coaching style, not the other way around. Whether you prefer a detailed daily breakdown or a broader monthly overview, CoachRx is equipped to meet your needs.

- **Enhanced Organization:** Tailor your calendar view to align with your organization style for more efficient program design.
- **Focused Design:** Choose the focus of your calendar view to streamline your design process, whether it’s exercise, lifestyle, or a mix of both.
- **Screen Space Optimization:** Customize the amount of information displayed on your screen to reduce clutter and enhance focus.
