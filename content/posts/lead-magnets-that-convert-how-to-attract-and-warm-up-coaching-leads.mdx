### What Is a Lead Magnet, Really?

It’s not “just a freebie.”

A lead magnet is a **high-value resource** you offer in exchange for someone’s email (or phone number) so you can follow up and build a relationship over time.

It should solve a specific problem, feel *worth it*, and help someone experience a preview of what it’s like to work with you.

It’s not fluff. It’s trust-building.

> Without a lead magnet, you’re not building a system —  
>  **You’re just posting and hoping.**

### Where This Fits in Your Marketing System

Let’s zoom out for a second:

In Episode 1, we walked through a 5-part marketing system. Here's how your lead magnet fits into it:

- Your **Core Weekly Content** brings visibility and education
- Your **Lead Magnet** gives someone a clear next step
- Your **Nurture System** builds trust and guides them toward booking a consult

A lead magnet is the *bridge* between attention and action.

It turns viewers into subscribers. Followers into leads. And conversations into consults.

### What Makes a Great Lead Magnet?

Here’s the shift:

We’ve moved past the “10 tips” PDFs and random checklists.

Today’s best-performing lead magnets are:

- Actionable
- Relevant
- Easy to consume
- Actually *useful*
- A preview of your coaching experience

**People don’t want one more thing to collect — they want real help.**  
They want micro mastery. Clarity. Momentum.

And your lead magnet can give them that.

### 5 Lead Magnet Formats That Work in 2024

Here are 5 simple, effective formats for coaches — choose one that fits your strengths and your offer:

#### 1. Video or Audio Lesson

Teach one thing. Share your voice, energy, and approach. Show what it’s like to be coached by you.

#### 2. Email Mini-Course

3–5 emails. One insight or shift per day. Helps someone feel supported and builds trust over time.

#### 3. Tool, Tracker, or Self-Audit

Let them assess where they are, track progress, or gain a key insight. Super useful for habit or behavior-based coaching.

#### 4. Short Guided Coaching Experience

A “5-Day Reset,” a quick challenge, or a structured series. Great for re-engaging warm leads or growing your list during a promo.

#### 5. Resource Vault or Coaching Preview Hub

Curated videos, PDFs, or links that showcase how you work. Think: Notion page, private Google Drive folder, or simple link list.

#### **BONUS**: Personalized Hybrid Format

High-trust, high-impact.

Ask someone to fill out a short form, and reply with a personalized Loom video + relevant resource.

This works best when you’re selling high-touch coaching — and it creates connection fast.

### Tools to Create and Deliver

You don’t need to be a tech wizard. Use what’s simple and accessible:

- **Create with:** Google Docs, Loom, Canva, Notion
- **Deliver with:** ConvertKit, MailerLite, Beehiiv, or even your CoachRx integrations

And be sure to include a **clear next step** — like booking a consult.

Tip: Let people know exactly what happens on that consult call.  
 What they’ll learn, how long it takes, and why it’s a smart move for them — even if they’re not ready to commit.

### Your Action Step

Don’t overthink it.

Pick one format. Create a simple version this week. Link it in your bio or attach it to your next post. A basic-but-helpful lead magnet will always outperform the “perfect” one you never finish.
