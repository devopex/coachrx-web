### Unlock the Power of Easy, Integrated Payments with CoachRx and Stripe

As a coach, managing your business efficiently is as crucial as delivering solid training programs. That's where CoachRx, in partnership with Stripe, steps in to provide you with an EASY wayto handle payments. Serving over 40 countries, this integration is designed to simplify collecting payments, allowing you to focus on what you do best…coaching.

#### Setting Up for Success: Easy Stripe Connection

Getting started with Stripe in CoachRx is simple and literally takes less time than it takes to write a few days of training. Remember, only the account owner can connect the Stripe account, but super admins can access the business suite features.

#### How to Connect:

1. Navigate to the business tab in CoachRx.
2. Click on 'Payments' and proceed to create your CoachRx Stripe account.
3. If you already have a Stripe account, use a different email for this setup.
4. Complete the Stripe registration process.
5. Once set up, you're ready to accept payments through Stripe.

#### What You Have Access to After Set-Up:

- Payments: Latest activities, invoices, and product creation.
- Recurring Subscriptions: Manage and automatically charge clients right through the platform.
- Metrics and Reporting: Detailed business insights including revenue, subscriptions, client data, and more.
- Payroll: Manage and understand coach payments with ease.
- Contracts and Waivers: Get rid of platforms like HelloSign and DocuSign and use CoachRx.
- Storefront: Create and customize your business’s online presence…and sell your products, subscriptions, and programs through your storefront.
- Customers: A consolidated view of all your clients and customers.

Integrating Stripe with CoachRx not only streamlines your payment processes but also opens doors to a variety of business management features.

Whether it's handling subscriptions, selling products, or analyzing business metrics, CoachRx and Stripe make it easier for you to focus on delivering exceptional coaching while efficiently managing your business.
