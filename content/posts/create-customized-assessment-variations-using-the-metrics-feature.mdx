#### **Pro Tip: Create Customized Assessment Variations using the Metrics Feature**

Movement screens and assessments are key to understanding your clients’ strengths and weaknesses. By leveraging these tools, you can tailor training programs that cater to individual needs, ensuring effective and efficient progress. With CoachRx, you can now create customized assessment variations using the Metrics feature, providing you with precise data to enhance your coaching strategy.

**Benefit:**

Incorporating movement screens into your practice offers numerous benefits:

- **Injury Prevention**: By identifying limitations that may increase the risk of injury, you can adopt a more proactive approach to injury prevention.
- **Program Design**: Develop programs that include corrective exercises to enhance fundamental movement patterns.
- **Progress Monitoring**: Keep track of your clients’ progress and movement development over time.
- **Exercise Selection**: Pinpoint strengths and weaknesses, allowing for more appropriate exercise choices based on individual levels.

**Steps to Use:**

1. Navigate to the Metrics tab located within your Index
2. Select Add Metric Group
3. Enter the Metric GroupName and Description
4. Add a variation of Metrics and unit of measurement.
5. Assign Metric group the relevant clients.
6. Start tracking progress!
