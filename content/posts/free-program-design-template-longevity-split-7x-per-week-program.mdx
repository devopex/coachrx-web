#### **Longevity Split (7x per week) Program**

#### **Why the Longevity Split Matters**

**Quality of Life Enhancement:** This program is built on longevity-biased principles, aiming to improve overall life quality through functional strength and solid aerobic capacity.

**Daily Intentional Movement:** By encouraging daily activity, the program fosters consistency and makes fitness a natural part of everyday life.

**Balanced Approach:** Combining resistance training with varied aerobic work ensures comprehensive fitness development.

**Sustainability:** The program's design prioritizes long-term adherence over short-term intensity, making it ideal for clients seeking lifelong fitness.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**7-Day Split:** Daily movement is key, with a mix of resistance training and aerobic work throughout the week.

**Variety in Exercise Selection:** This approach not only prevents boredom but also ensures a well-rounded fitness development.

**Intensity Management:** The program balances challenging workouts with easier sessions, promoting recovery and preventing burnout.

#### **Weekly Schedule Breakdown**

1. **Monday:** Full Body Resistance

- Focus: Functional strength training targeting all major muscle groups

2. **Tuesday:** Easy Aerobic

- Focus: Low-intensity steady-state cardio for base building and recovery

3. **Wednesday:** Full Body Resistance

- Focus: Varied resistance exercises to build and maintain strength

4. **Thursday:** Easy Aerobic

- Focus: Another session of low-impact cardiovascular work

5. **Friday:** Full Body Resistance

- Focus: Final resistance session of the week, reinforcing functional strength

6. **Saturday:** Tough Aerobic

- Focus: Higher-intensity aerobic work to challenge cardiovascular fitness

7. **Sunday:** Easy Aerobic (outdoors)

- Focus: Light activity, preferably in nature, promoting active recovery and mental wellbeing

This carefully planned schedule ensures a perfect balance between strength development, cardiovascular health, and recovery, all while promoting daily movement.

#### **The Science Behind Longevity-Focused Training**

**Functional Strength:** Regular resistance training maintains muscle mass and bone density, crucial for independence in later years.

**Cardiovascular Health:** Consistent aerobic work improves heart health, reduces the risk of chronic diseases, and enhances overall endurance.

**Neuroplasticity:** Varied movement patterns and exercises promote brain health and cognitive function.

**Hormonal Balance:** Regular, moderate exercise helps maintain healthy hormone levels, supporting overall wellbeing.
