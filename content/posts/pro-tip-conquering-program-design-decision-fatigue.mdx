Ever found yourself staring at a blank programming calendar for your 15th client of the day, your brain completely flatlined? That mental exhaustion you're feeling has a name: decision fatigue. It's a real psychological phenomenon that affects even the most experienced coaches, and it can seriously impact both your program quality and your mental wellbeing.

#### **Pro Tip: Conquering Program Design Decision Fatigue**

#### **What is Decision Fatigue?**

Decision fatigue occurs when the quality of your decisions deteriorates after making many consecutive choices. For coaches, program design involves hundreds of micro-decisions:

- Selecting appropriate movement patterns
- Choosing specific exercises for each pattern
- Determining sets, reps, tempo, and rest periods
- Creating effective exercise pairings
- Planning progression strategies
- Accommodating individual limitations

When multiplied across your entire client roster, you're making thousands of decisions weekly—a recipe for mental exhaustion that can lead to procrastination, second-guessing, or falling back on overly rigid templates.
