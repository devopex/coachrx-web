### Program Design Pro Tip: **Tailor Your Coaching with Custom Workout Visibility!**

Understanding and adapting to each client’s unique needs is essential for effective coaching. At CoachRx, we provide you with tools like customizable workout visibility settings to tailor each client's experience, fostering better engagement and success.

No two individuals are the same, and acknowledging and addressing their distinct needs is pivotal in fostering a trusting and productive coaching relationship. This personalized approach not only cultivates a sense of understanding and empathy but also lays the groundwork for achieving optimal results. CoachRx's visibility settings offer a dynamic platform for tailoring the coaching experience to match the preferences and requirements of each of your clients.

**Program Design Pro Tip: How To Adjust Workout Visibility Settings**

Different clients require different levels of engagement. Some may feel overwhelmed by seeing their entire week of workouts, while others might thrive on that overview to better plan their week.

Imagine having the ability to seamlessly adapt the visibility of workouts to align with your clients' comfort levels and schedules. For those who prefer a more gradual approach, providing visibility for one workout at a time can alleviate feelings of overwhelm and promote focus and clarity. Conversely, clients with hectic schedules can benefit from a comprehensive overview of their weekly workouts, empowering them to plan and prioritize their training effectively.

#### **Here’s how to customize workout visibility settings for your clients:**

1. **Access Client Profile:** Go to the client’s profile and scroll to the visibility settings.
2. **Customize Visibility Options:** Choose how far in advance clients can see their workouts. Options range from seeing a specified number of day’s workout to viewing future workouts within the current week   \*note that the current week is Monday-Sunday
3. **Save Your Settings:** Ensure all changes are saved correctly to reflect across the client's workouts.
