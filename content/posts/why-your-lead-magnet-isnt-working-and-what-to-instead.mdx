If you’ve got a folder of half-finished PDFs, a blinking cursor in Canva, or a “great idea” that never quite becomes real, this one’s for you.

Your lead magnet isn’t “just a freebie.” In a trust-based coaching business, it’s the first moment of impact: a tiny, real win that lets someone *experience* your method.

Done right, it becomes the bridge from your content → to your coaching philosophy → to a consult.

I made a simple, fillable [worksheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fpM8dTUeizTlAwXB5X9il2y4ZFLKaD7K1Xmw5i0w9Pc/edit?usp=sharing) to help you build yours in under 30 minutes.

#### Why This Matters (And Why Most Coaches Miss)

Most lead magnets fail because they’re:

- **Too broad** (“Better health in 30 days!”)
- **Solving the wrong problem** (not what your client is actively searching for)
- **Disconnected from your actual coaching** (great design, zero alignment)

Your lead magnet should be **helpful and relevant** to what you truly do—so it earns permission for the next step.

**Think:** first dose of your coaching, not a random freebie.

#### Anchor It to Your Coaching Content Signature

If you’ve listened to Ep 24 and Ep 27, you know your **Coaching Content Signature** (beliefs, POV, style, transformational topics) is your north star. Use it here.

Ask:

1. **Belief Shift:** What mindset reframe must happen before someone succeeds with me?
2. **Micro-Problem:** What specific, repeatable problem do I solve early in the journey?
3. **Unique Lens:** How is my approach different, clearer, or more doable?

If it doesn’t express your signature, it won’t feel uniquely “you.” And if it isn’t uniquely you, it’s forgettable.

#### Choose the Right Topic (The 10PM Test)

**Would my ideal client Google this at 10pm in a moment of stress or urgency?** If yes, you’re close.

Aim for **specific + urgent + relevant** to your paid offer.

**Name formulas:**

- **How to [tiny transformation] for [specific person]**
- **Free Guide: [Desirable outcome] Without [common frustration]**

**Examples:**

- *New-Parent Evening Routine: 7-Min Checklist for Deeper Sleep & Next-Day Energy*
- *Protein Without Tracking: 10 Plug-and-Play Meals for Busy Women Who Hate MyFitnessPal*

One problem. One outcome. One reader.

#### Build It Fast and Focused (1 Page + 1 Short Video)

You do **not** need an ebook. You need a **tool people use.**

**Suggested structure (1 page):**

1. **What this is / who it’s for / how to use it** (3 lines)
2. **3–5 steps or swaps** (clear, testable)
3. **Next step CTA** (book consult, reply to email, or watch your deeper lesson)

Optionally add a **2–5 minute loom/video** showing how to apply it. Think tiny demo, not a course.

**Pro tip:** Don’t call it an “ebook.” Call it a **Blueprint, Swipe File, Checklist, Toolkit**—and make sure it behaves like one.

#### Promote It Like a Coaching Asset (Not a Secret)

If no one sees it, it can’t work.

**Baseline promo:**

- Link in bio/Linktree
- Weekly Story or Reel mention (clip your “why this matters”)
- Show notes + companion blogs
- Auto-reply trigger: “DM me **[keyword]**”

**Level up:**

- Short welcome video on the thank-you page outlining the **next step**
- 3-email nurture (deliver → deepen → invite)
- Invite replies and send a quick Loom back

#### Common Mistakes (And the Fix)

1. **Too broad** → Narrow to one outcome.
2. **Wrong problem** → Use client language from DMs, consults, check-ins.
3. **Overdelivering** → Solve one thing; don’t dump your whole system.
4. **No next step** → Add a clear CTA that matches your offer.
5. **No follow-up** → Send 2–3 simple emails; ask for a reply.

#### Mini-Workshop: Build Yours Now (5 Prompts)

Open a doc and answer:

1. **Who is it for (1 line)?**
2. **One problem it solves (1 sentence)?**
3. **Three steps/swaps someone can do today (bullets)?**
4. **How they’ll know it worked (1 metric/marker)?**
5. **What to do next (one clear CTA)?**

Format to one page. Record a 2–5 minute walkthrough. Ship.

You can also use the worksheet I made you. [Grab it here.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fpM8dTUeizTlAwXB5X9il2y4ZFLKaD7K1Xmw5i0w9Pc/edit?usp=sharing)

#### Launch Checklist

- One-page tool finished (editable + PDF)
- 2–5 min video recorded
- Landing page + thank-you video
- Nurture 1–3 scheduled
- Bio/link + weekly Stories/Clips queued
- “DM **KEYWORD**” automation set

#### Where This Lives in Your System

- **Weekly anchor** introduces the problem and invites to the tool.
- **Daily clips** hit the pain, show a quick win, and point to the tool.
- **Lead magnet** delivers a first result → **email** deepens trust → **consult** closes like coaching.
