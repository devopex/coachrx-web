#### WHAT IS THE PROGRAMS FEATURE?

```
Programs is a feature in CoachRx that allows you to design, sell and coach less specialized, less individualized programs with fixed or real-time programming.
```

```
And while individual design is centered around a relationship with a coach and client, Programs is centered around the program itself and utilizes an avatar to drive the planning and program design process. As a result, these training programs will be more generalized than specialized, but they can still be valuable to a motivated client with specific goals. 
```

```
For example: Programs allows you to design a training program for a person looking to get their first muscle-up, a person training for their first 10 km run, or for a new mother wanting to get back into fitness after giving birth. Or, these programs can be less specific than the latter, such as a 12-week full body strength program designed for anyone looking to increase their strength.
```

```
You can also leverage Programs to build out common progressions or splits to assign and personalize for your individual design clients (like an upper/lower split or 5k row progression), helping you cut down on programming time. 
```

```
Bonus: If you already sell templates or group programs, you can start using CoachRx and continue to sell and deliver these programs seamlessly.
