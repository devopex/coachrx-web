#### **Cluster Sets For Strength**

We’re thrilled to announce another addition to the CoachRx program library: **Cluster Sets for Strength**. This program is designed for coaches who are committed to pushing their clients' strength boundaries by leveraging the power of cluster sets.

This program uses cluster sets to build strength in the Back Squat, Multi-Grip Bench Press, Trap Bar Deadlift, and Weighted Pull-Up. These exercises are the priority exercises in the design with accessory, or secondary, exercises to follow each day.

The program follows an Upper/Lower layout to allow for focus and recovery between sessions. In terms of progression, you will notice that the primary exercises are decreasing in total volume every 2 weeks with the intention of slightly more intensity each week. The accessory exercises stay static in total volume across all 6 weeks with a shift in exercise selection in week 4.

#### Why Cluster Sets Matter Targeted Strength Building

**Targeted Strength Building:** The program focuses on key exercises such as the Back Squat, Multi-Grip Bench Press, Trap Bar Deadlift, and Weighted Pull-Up, ensuring your clients develop strength in critical muscle groups.

**Structured for Progress:** The well-planned Upper/Lower layout ensures that each session provides optimal focus and recovery, allowing your clients to train effectively without overexertion.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**Progressive Intensity:** The primary exercises are designed to reduce in total volume every two weeks, with each session aiming for increased intensity. This progressive overload approach ensures continuous strength gains.

**Strategic Accessory Work:** Accessory exercises maintain a consistent volume, with a shift in exercises at week four to provide variety and targeted muscle engagement. This keeps the training dynamic and prevents plateaus.
