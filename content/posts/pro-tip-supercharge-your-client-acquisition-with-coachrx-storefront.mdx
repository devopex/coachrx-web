#### Why This Matters

In today's digital age, clients crave personalized solutions and seamless purchasing experiences. Your expertise deserves a showcase that not only highlights your unique offerings but also makes it irresistibly easy for potential clients to say "Yes!" to your services.

### Introducing CoachRx Storefront: Your 24/7 Sales Machine

**What it does:** Creates a professional, customizable online shop for your coaching services.

**Key Benefits:**

- Showcase your programs 24/7
- Streamline the purchase process
- Build trust with testimonials
- Offer diverse product types (workouts, nutrition, hybrid packages)

### Your Step-by-Step Guide to Storefront Success

1. **Connect to Stripe:** Ensure seamless payments
2. **Set Up Products:** Link your offerings to storefront pages
3. **Create Your Storefront:**

   - Navigate to "Storefront" in Business Suite
   - Click "Create" and choose Organization or Coach storefront
   - Craft compelling copy, add eye-catching images and videos
   - Set page to "Live" and save
4. **Customize Your URL:**

   - Click the 3 dots next to your storefront
   - Choose "Edit" to access your unique URL
5. **Add Product Offerings:**

   - Return to Storefront home page
   - Click 3 dots and select "Edit Product Offerings"
   - Create product buckets and add detailed descriptions
6. **Populate Your Store:**

   - Click 3 dots next to product offering and "Edit Products"
   - Create products with names, descriptions, and linked plans
   - Repeat for all your amazing offerings!

### Pro Tips for a Standout Storefront

1. **Highlight Benefits:** Focus on outcomes, not just features
2. **Use Social Proof:** Sprinkle in client testimonials and success stories
3. **Clear CTAs:** Make it obvious how to purchase with clear "Buy Now" buttons
4. **Diverse Offerings:** Include a mix of entry-level and premium services
5. **Regular Updates:** Keep your storefront fresh with new programs and seasonal offers

### Your 7-Day Storefront Launch Plan

1. **Day 1-2:** Set up Stripe and outline your product offerings
2. **Day 3-4:** Create and design your storefront page
3. **Day 5:** Add products and write compelling descriptions
4. **Day 6:** Gather and add testimonials
5. **Day 7:** Review, test purchase process, and go live!
