We're thrilled to announce a significant upgrade to the CoachRx Mobile App, designed to enhance your coaching experience and keep you connected with your clients wherever you are. Our improved coach interface in light mode brings a new level of efficiency and engagement to your fingertips.

![OPEX July Feature Update- Coach Mobile App Experience](/images/content/9182a0ac44-opex-july-feature-update-coach-mobile-app-experience-1x1-1.webp)

#### **What's New in the Enhanced Coach Experience?**

Our updated mobile app makes it easier than ever to manage your coaching business on-the-go. Here's what you can do with the improved interface:

1. 📲 **Message Clients**: Stay in touch with real-time messaging, including video capabilities.
2. 💬 **Comment on Workouts**: Provide timely feedback and encouragement.
3. 🔔 **View Notifications**: Stay on top of client activities and important alerts.
4. 📊 **View & Enter Workout Results**: Track progress and update information instantly.
5. 😍 **Use Emoji Reactions**: Add a personal touch to your interactions.

#### **A Comprehensive Coaching Tool at Your Fingertips**

The enhanced coach app provides a quick view of your client list and allows you to:

- Review training and lifestyle information
- View and comment on workouts, lifestyle choices, and weekly check-ins as they're completed
- Get alerted to any due Rx's

#### **Communication at the Heart of Coaching**

We understand that your greatest asset as a coach is your ability to communicate and build real relationships with your clients. That's why we've put communication front and center in our app design. Unlike other apps where communication feels like an afterthought, CoachRx prioritizes your connection with clients through:

- Real-time messaging with video capabilities
- Push notifications to keep you informed
- Emoji reactions for quick, engaging responses

#### **Seamless Coach-Client Toggle**

For coaches who are also clients themselves, we've made it easy to switch between interfaces. Simply use the settings in your profile screen to toggle between your coach and client accounts, allowing you to focus on the functionalities you need at any given moment.
