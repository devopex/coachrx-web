#### **Getting Started: Syncing Client Devices**

The first step to smarter Fitness, Nutrition, & Lifestyle Coaching is easy. Clients use the health trackers and apps they already have. They sync any device or health kit with CoachRx no need for new tech.

On your coach dashboard, you’ll see right away which device is linked to each client. The feature starts pulling data from whatever they connect, even if you haven’t set specific lifestyle goals yet.

**Common data sources include:**

- **Readiness:** Pulled from Oura Ring
- **Movement:** Tracked using Apple Health
- **Nutrition:** Logged from Cronometer

CoachRx collects and organizes these streams, prepping a rich profile for each client.

#### **Navigating to Lifestyle Trends**

To view lifestyle trends for a client, just follow a few simple steps:

- Click into the client’s calendar.
- Expand the left sidebar.
- Select “Lifestyle Trends.”

For a bigger, cleaner look, close the left sidebar. Now you’ve got a full-screen view showing all lifestyle data tracked by the client’s wearables, regardless of any lifestyle prescriptions.

#### **Viewing Tracked Data in Detail**

Once you’re in the Lifestyle Trends view, you’ll see every tracked metric from the client’s wearables and heath data integrations without guessing about where the data’s coming from.

You’ll always know the *data source.* For example, you might see that:

- Readiness data comes via Oura.
- Movement is sourced from Apple Health.
- Nutrition is tracked through Cronometer.

CoachRx draws this data into clear trend graphs, letting coaches spot patterns, spikes, or dips without confusion.

#### **Exploring Time-Based Views**

Coaches can see trends over different time frames to spot patterns or sudden changes. Switch between:

- **Day view:** Instant feedback on last 24 hours.
- **Week view:** Track progress or setbacks over a standard week.
- **Month view:** Big-picture trends and long-term shifts.

Use the day view to pinpoint sharp changes, the week view to track improvement, and the month view to see how small habits add up.

#### **Customizing Your Trends Dashboard**

Every client is unique. That’s why you can pick which trends show up on their dashboard. Turn individual metrics on or off, whether you want to focus on sleep, movement, or something else.

When you’re set, **hit save configuration to lock in your view**. Each setup stays until you change it, so every dashboard reflects that client’s specific goals and needs. The more relevant the info, the easier coaching becomes.

#### **What Are Insights? An Overview**

Insights appear automatically once the platform collects enough client data. These instant highlights make it much easier to spot what’s going well—or where help is needed.

They’re meant for fast, smarter coaching decisions, taking you right to the important changes.

> “Sleep, HRV, and readiness all have improved this week.”

![](/images/content/d530701bce-screenshot-2025-09-15-at-11-07-52-am.webp)

#### **Sample Insight: Positive Trends**

Suppose you spot an insight like improved sleep, higher HRV (heart rate variability), and better readiness. Clicking it pulls up a panel with more details:

- Which metrics improved and by how much.
- A quick coaching suggestion, like “Consider progressing training or checking readiness for new goals.”
- An easy connection to targeted adjustments you can make.

This direct feedback moves you immediately from data to action.

#### **Handling Negative Trends with Insights**

If you see something like *readiness trending down*, that’s the signal to adjust. Click the insight and you’ll get advice like:

- Lower training volume for recovery.
- Explore sleep or stress strategies.
- Consider a rest week or less intensity.

These insights respond directly to the data, so you always know if the client is thriving or struggling, and what to do next.

*Trending down* means time to act.

**Possible responses:**

- Reduce training intensity.
- Prescribe new sleep habits.
- Open a candid chat about recovery.

#### **Tracking Readiness Over Time**

Think of readiness as a moving target. You could see a +16 gain from one day to the next, then a slow drop for a few days, a jump, and then another dip. The visual graph lets you watch these *fluctuations* play out.

Real-time trend tracking helps you make the right calls when to encourage harder work or when to insist on rest.

#### **Diving into Nutrition Trends**

Nutrition impacts everything, energy, mood, recovery. With CoachRx, you can review daily intake for protein, carbs, and fats. These numbers are pulled from nutrition apps like Cronometer.

Picture color-coded graphs: various shades of green represent fiber, protein, carbs, and fat. Imbalances stand out clearly, making it easy to see when adjustments are needed for better progress or recovery.

#### **Understanding Grayed-Out Graphs**

If you see a grayed-out chart, it just means data isn’t being collected yet. Most often, clients need to sync a device or their device doesn’t track that metric.

**Quick troubleshooting tips:**

- Make sure a compatible device is connected.
- Double-check app permissions.

As clients settle in, those gray spots usually fill up with data.

**Prescribe to get back on track.** Use trends to fine-tune program changes as soon as trouble appears.
