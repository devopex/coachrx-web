### **What It Is:**

The enhanced Lifestyle Rx feature has been completely redesigned to streamline your workflow. You can now create lifestyle prescriptions directly from your client's calendar AND leverage sophisticated tracking metrics that go far beyond simple yes/no completion.

### **How to Use It:**

#### Step 1: Access the Lifestyle Calendar

- Open your client's calendar
- Look for the two tabs at the top: "Exercise" and "Lifestyle"
- Click on "Lifestyle" to view the lifestyle-specific calendar
![](/images/content/af0ff162fd-1.webp)

#### Step 2: Create Prescriptions Directly on the Calendar

- Click on any day in the calendar
- Select the broccoli icon to add a lifestyle prescription
- Choose from the expanded list of lifestyle types (steps, active time, bed time, bowel movement number, calories, chews, exercise time, macronutrients, micronutrients, sleep hours, sleep quality)
![](/images/content/3d16d40369-2.webp)

#### **Step 3: Set Detailed Tracking Parameters**

- Enter your instructions for the client (e.g., "Aim to get 8-10,000 steps daily")
- Set specific targets with customizable ranges (e.g., 10,000 steps with a +/- 2,000 range)
- Schedule reminder notifications to prompt client tracking (e.g., 8:00 PM daily)

#### Step 4: Create Recurring Prescriptions

- Choose from flexible scheduling options: no repeat, daily, specific days
- Set the prescription to repeat for up to 12 weeks (expanded from the previous 4-week limit)
- Add multiple prescriptions by clicking the blue box and selecting "add prescription"
![](/images/content/65c081aa86-3.webp)

### **Why It Matters:**

This integrated approach transforms how you design and manage client lifestyle prescriptions:

- Time Efficiency: Create prescriptions in seconds, directly from the calendar interface
- Better Client Compliance: Set custom reminders that prompt clients at optimal times
- Longer Programming Cycles: Extend prescriptions up to 12 weeks for more consistent habit formation
- Detailed Data Collection: Track specific metrics like target ranges rather than simple yes/no completion
- Visual Progress Monitoring: See compliance patterns at a glance on the calendar view

Remember, the OPEX philosophy emphasizes that transformation happens during all 24 hours of the day—not just during training sessions. These enhanced tools help you influence those critical hours by creating truly personalized daily habits tailored to each client's unique needs.
