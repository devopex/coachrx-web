"Assess, don't guess." - James FitzGerald's mantra cuts straight to the heart of professional coaching. Why? Because assessment offers you the truth – and in an industry full of quick fixes and guesswork, truth is your greatest asset as a coach.

#### The Power of Professional Assessment

In today's fitness landscape, where quick fixes and generic programs dominate, systematic assessment sets professional coaches apart. The CoachRx assessment feature transforms OPEX's proven evaluation methodology into a powerful digital toolset that delivers actionable insights for every client.

#### Understanding the Assessment Framework

The CoachRx assessment feature captures three essential components that form a complete picture of your client's current status:

**OPEX Body**

Track comprehensive body composition metrics including:

- Body fat percentage measurements for accurate composition tracking
- Lean body mass calculations to guide programming decisions
- BMR assessments for metabolic understanding
- Skeletal muscle mass evaluation for strength potential

**OPEX Move**

Evaluate six foundational movement patterns crucial for program design:

- Upper body push and pull capabilities
- Squat mechanics and limitations
- Lunge pattern assessment
- Hinge movement evaluation
- Core stability and control
- Mobility screening results

**OPEX Work**

Measure work capacity and aerobic sustainability through:

- Standardized testing protocols
- 10-minute air bike assessment
- Recovery rate evaluation
- Work output tracking
![](/images/content/b32bd35623-screenshot-2024-12-13-at-8-10-52-am.webp)

#### Three Steps to Assessment Mastery

**1. Navigate & Input**

Start your assessment journey with these key steps:

- Access the assessment feature from the left sidebar after selecting your client
- Input detailed body composition metrics in OPEX Body
- Document movement pattern evaluations in OPEX Move
- Record comprehensive work capacity test results in OPEX Work

**2. Review & Analyze**

Transform data into actionable insights:

- Use the automatically generated priority list in the sidebar
- Identify immediate focus areas - your "lowest hanging fruit"
- Create a clear hierarchy of priorities based on findings
- Connect assessment results to program design strategy

**3. Apply & Monitor**

Turn insights into results:

- Let your priority list guide program design decisions
- Share clear, documented findings with clients
- Set realistic timelines based on assessment data
- Schedule regular reassessments to track progress

#### Why Professional Assessment Matters

As James FitzGerald emphasizes, "Clients love the truth because they get to see what you do is personalized and they're on the right path. It's an opportunity to build trust."

Your assessment data serves multiple crucial purposes:

- Creates a comprehensive baseline beyond basic movement screening
- Identifies specific strengths and weaknesses across all fitness domains
- Enables data-driven goal setting and progression
- Provides concrete metrics for progress tracking
- Builds client confidence in your professional approach
- Transforms program design from guesswork into strategic planning

#### Professional Implementation Tips

1. Schedule adequate time for initial assessments
2. Create a consistent assessment workflow
3. Document observations thoroughly
4. Use assessment results to educate clients
5. Build reassessment timelines into your coaching calendar

#### Remember

Without understanding your client's starting point in terms of body composition, movement patterns, and work capacity, your design is simply guesswork. The assessment process isn't just about gathering data – it's about establishing yourself as a professional who creates highly personalized programs based on objective findings rather than assumptions.
