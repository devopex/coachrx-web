#### Postpartum Resistance & Aerobics (4 Week)

#### **Why Postpartum Resistance & Aerobics Matters**

**Targeted Recovery:** The program focuses on core and pelvic stability and endurance, crucial areas for post-partum recovery.

**Holistic Approach:** By combining resistance training with easy aerobics, the program supports overall fitness restoration.

**Gradual Progression:** The linear design allows new moms to ease back into exercise safely and confidently.

**Emphasis on Self-Regulation:** The program encourages women to listen to their bodies and focus on consistency rather than intensity.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**4-Week Structure:** The program provides a month-long framework for re-establishing a fitness routine.

**3 Weekly Sessions:** Balanced workouts that combine resistance training and aerobic work.

**Core Focus:** Each session includes specific core work to rebuild abdominal and pelvic floor strength.

**Motor Control Development:** Exercises are chosen to enhance motor control across all movement patterns.

#### **Weekly Schedule Breakdown**

1. **Monday:**

- Resistance: Squat, Bend, Push, Core
- Cardio: Easy aerobic - mixed cyclical
- Focus: Lower body strength, pushing movements, and varied low-intensity cardio

2. **Wednesday:**

- Resistance: Bend, Pull, Push, Core
- Cardio: Easy aerobic - cyclical
- Focus: Upper body emphasis with continued core work and steady-state cardio

3. **Friday:**

- Resistance: Lunge, Pull, Push, Core
- Cardio: Easy aerobic - cyclical
- Focus: Lower body variation, upper body balance, and consistent cardio approach

This carefully planned schedule ensures a balanced approach to post-partum fitness, addressing all major movement patterns while prioritizing core strength and gentle cardiovascular conditioning.

#### **The Science Behind Post-Partum Exercise**

**Core Rehabilitation:** Focused core work helps restore abdominal strength and function, crucial for posture and daily activities.

**Pelvic Floor Recovery:** Targeted exercises support pelvic floor rehabilitation, addressing common post-partum concerns.

**Hormonal Considerations:** The program's moderate intensity aligns with the hormonal fluctuations common in the post-partum period.

**Psychological Benefits:** Regular exercise can help combat post-partum depression and boost overall mood and energy levels.
