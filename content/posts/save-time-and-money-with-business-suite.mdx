#### WHAT IS THE BUSINESS SUITE?

```
The Business Suite feature is designed to handle all the business-related tasks you need to do—tasks that are generally spread out between various software systems, apps or platforms.
```

```
In short, Business Suite consolidates everything you need to run your business into one place—the CoachRx app—thus helping you manage and grow your business seamlessly in cost and time-saving ways, all the while providing clients with a more professional experience. 
```

```

```

#### HOW YOU CAN USE IT

```
One of the most important ways you can use Business Suite is by taking advantage of its ability to provide you vital business performance indicators, such as gross revenue, net revenue, total subscriptions, average client value, as well as things like past due invoices that might otherwise go unnoticed.
```

```
Seeing this data couldn’t be any easier. You simply login, and (as long as you’re keeping good books) voila, it’s right there on your dashboard in plain view.
```

```
Further, you can use Business Suite to manage, for example, your customer list, update billing information or view invoice and subscription history for each client, thus acting as CRM software right in the app. 
```

```
The bottom line is, if you’re looking to scale your business at all, you need to have a deep understanding of all of the important business and client metrics, and Business Suite makes this especially easy for you.
```

```
Taking payments—be it a one-time payment or recurring monthly payments—via Business Suite is made easy through its ability to integrate with other Stripe software. It’s as simple as creating a Stripe account, adding products and subscriptions, and then collecting payments from clients.
