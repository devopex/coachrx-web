Ever notice how clients who train remotely are often the first to drop off? Creating that "in-person feel" when you're miles apart isn't just nice to have—it's essential for client retention and results.

The coaches who thrive in today's fitness landscape are those who can deliver exceptional experiences regardless of physical location—and CoachRx gives you the structure to do exactly that.

### **What It Is:**

High-touch remote coaching is a systematic approach to client communication that creates consistency, builds trust, and delivers personalized feedback—even when you can't be physically present. At its core, it's about creating structured touchpoints that make clients feel seen, supported, and successful through three key components:

1. Daily Workout Feedback - Simple acknowledgments that you're monitoring progress
2. Weekly Check-In Systems - Structured questionnaires to spot potential issues early
3. Monthly Consultations - Deeper conversations to maintain the coaching relationship

### **How to Use It:**

Let me walk you through implementing this system in CoachRx:

#### Step 1: Set Up Your Touchpoint System

- Navigate to your Organization settings where you'll find the Touchpoints feature
- Set your baseline touchpoint goal (I recommend at least 1-2 per client per week)
- Create a consistent schedule for checking client workouts (e.g., morning clients before lunch)

\*\*Coach tip: You don't need to write novels! Even simple emoji reactions to completed workouts count as touchpoints. The key is consistency, not length.

![](/images/content/b102df2dd5-1.webp)

#### Step 2: Leverage Video Messaging

- Use the Loom integration in four key ways:

- Exercise demonstrations for new movements
- Movement feedback on client videos
- Personalized responses to weekly check-ins
- Workout overview videos in your coach's notes
![](/images/content/e06a9444aa-2.webp)

#### Step 3: Create Supportive Educational Resources

- Add educational documents to your Index section
- Create client guides for common questions and issues
- Set clear communication expectations and "office hours"
![](/images/content/e05d9012d5-3.webp)

### **Why It Matters:**

This system doesn't just improve the client experience—it transforms your business. When you master remote coaching:

- You can work with ideal clients regardless of geography
- Your business isn't dependent on local market conditions
- You can scale without requiring more physical presence
- You differentiate yourself in an increasingly saturated market

As James FitzGerald often emphasizes, coaching is about relationship-building first, programming second. These systematic touchpoints are what bridge the physical gap and create the trust necessary for clients to follow your guidance long-term.

This week's challenge: Implement just ONE element of this system and observe how it impacts client engagement. Start small, stay consistent, and watch your remote coaching relationships flourish.
