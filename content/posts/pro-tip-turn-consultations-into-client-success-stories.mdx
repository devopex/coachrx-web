First impressions matter - especially in coaching. A great consultation can set the foundation for years of successful coaching, while a disorganized one can doom the relationship before it begins. Today, I want to share how to create a consultation system that converts prospects and sets clear expectations from day one.

### **Pro Tip: Turn Consultations into Client Success Stories**

#### **What It Is:**

A comprehensive consultation management system in CoachRx that combines pre-consultation preparation, structured conversation frameworks, and professional follow-up documentation to create meaningful client connections.

#### How to Use It:

**Pre-Consultation Prep**

- Review client intake forms before meeting
- Note potential areas for deeper discussion
- Prepare conversation guide using NEPA framework:

- Notice: Observe client behavior and responses
- Explain: Ask clarifying questions
- Prescribe: Align services with needs
- Action: Agree on next steps

**During Consultation**

- Take notes in organized sections: • Client-facing notes (shared with client) • Private notes (coach observations)
- Document key information: • Goals and priorities • Previous experiences • Potential obstacles • Agreed-upon actions
![](/images/content/84bfd8765a-1.webp)

**Post-Consultation Follow-up**

- Export professional consultation summary
- Send to client via app or email
- Schedule follow-up consultations
- Set clear timeline for next steps
- Document expectations clearly
![](/images/content/5944197cf6-2.webp)

### Why It Matters:

#### This system transforms your coaching by:

- Creating clear expectations from day one
- Building trust through professionalism
- Preventing miscommunication
- Making follow-up consistent
- Creating accountable coaching relationships
- Providing reference points for future conversations
