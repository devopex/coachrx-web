Looking to scale your coaching business beyond one-on-one clients? Creating and selling online programs is a powerful way to increase your impact and revenue without adding more hours to your day. With CoachRx's program selling feature, you can transform your coaching expertise into digital products that reach more people while maintaining the principles of personalized fitness that make OPEX coaching so effective.

**Pro Tip: Create and Sell Programs Online with CoachRx**

**What are Sellable Programs?**

Sellable programs are packaged coaching offerings that allow clients to purchase and follow your programming without requiring the same level of personalization as your individual design clients. These could include:

- Specialized fitness programs (strength development, postpartum fitness, etc.)
- Sport-specific training cycles
- General fitness templates for different experience levels
- Lifestyle and nutrition protocols

Unlike fully individualized coaching, these programs allow you to leverage your expertise at scale while providing a more accessible price point for clients who may not be ready for premium individual design.

**How to Create and Sell Programs in CoachRx**

Follow these step-by-step instructions to turn your coaching knowledge into sellable products:

**1. Set Up Payment Processing**

- Navigate to the Business Suite
- Connect your Stripe account
- Verify payment processing capabilities

**2. Create Your Program Template**

- Go to Index → Programs
- Choose between standard or live program
- Design your program following OPEX principles
- Include proper progression based on training age
- Respect OPEX Gain, Pain, and Sustain ordering principles
- Balance movement patterns following FME guidelines
![](/images/content/190eebe7ea-1.webp)

**3. Connect Program to Product**

- In Business Suite, create a new product
- Set appropriate pricing tiers
- Link your program template to the product
- Establish billing cycles and duration
![](/images/content/61181082d6-2.webp)

**4. Customize Your Sales Page**

- Update program settings to customize the landing page
- Add compelling images and videos
- Write benefit-focused descriptions
- Include coach information to build credibility
- Highlight what makes your approach unique
- Add testimonials if available
![](/images/content/6ae54b6425-3.webp)

**5. Make Program Public and Share**

- Toggle the "Public" setting on
- Get your unique program URL
- Share via social media, email, or your website
- Consider promoting to past clients first

**6. Manage Client Experience**

- Set up automatic onboarding emails
- Create program-specific resources
- Establish communication expectations
- Consider program coach separation for scalability (optional)

#### **Why This Matters**

Creating sellable programs aligns perfectly with the OPEX philosophy of developing client autonomy. By building programs that teach proper progression and movement quality while honoring individualized approaches, you're helping more people access quality coaching.

For your business, sellable programs offer several advantages:

- Leverage Your Time: Create once, sell many times
- Build Passive Revenue: Programs continue selling while you focus on premium clients
- Create Client Progression: Offer an entry point for clients who may later upgrade to individual design
- Reach New Markets: Connect with clients regardless of geographic location
- Demonstrate Expertise: Showcase your knowledge in specific fitness domains

Remember that the most successful program creators still incorporate OPEX principles into their design - respecting assessment results, proper progression, and the balance of movement patterns. Your programs should reflect the same quality and attention to principles as your individual design coaching, just packaged for a broader audience.
