#### HOW THE COACHRX APP HELPS THE CLIENT

```
In a nutshell, the CoachRx app allows clients to seamlessly fill out their exercise and lifestyle prescriptions each day, keep their profiles up-to-date with any relevant  information, handle billing, schedule consultations, and communicate with their coach as needed.
```

```
In the Client Corner, clients can adjust their training schedule, fill out equipment lists, complete their intake form, view important documents, and track their progress with visual graphs for resistance and energy systems assessments. They can also view the most important success metric—their compliance to their exercise, lifestyle and nutrition prescriptions—to add a layer of accountability. 
```

```
Your clients are busy, and their fitness app should make it as easy as possible to build consistency. From the Daily Rx screen, the exercise and lifestyle Rx features are simple to follow and complete, and even provide customizable push notifications to remind your clients to track. Your client can also fill out a weekly check-in to share an overview of their training, nutrition, and lifestyle quality for the week (and you can even personalize the questions for each client). 
```

One of the biggest keys to the CoachRx app is that, unlike every other app on the market, this one is designed specifically to be able to accommodate individualized prescriptions, both exercise and lifestyle, including the ability to track client progress and compliance
