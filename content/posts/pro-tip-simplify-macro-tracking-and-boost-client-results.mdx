### Your Step-by-Step Guide to Macro Coaching Excellence

1. **Create the Plan**

   - Navigate to your client's calendar and click the 🥦 icon
   - Set the duration of the plan (e.g., 4 weeks)
   - Define macro goals, including flexibility parameters
   - Schedule daily reminders (8 PM is a popular choice!)
2. **Communicate Goals**

   - Clearly explain macro targets to your client
   - Ensure they understand and are committed to the plan
3. **Track Compliance Like a Pro**

   - Clients answer a simple 'Yes' or 'No' daily
   - They can upload screenshots of their tracking app for your review
   - This streamlined approach encourages consistency without overwhelming clients
4. **Adapt and Conquer**

   - Regularly review compliance and progress
   - Adjust macro goals as needed to keep clients progressing
   - Use the data to inform your coaching decisions and provide personalized feedback
