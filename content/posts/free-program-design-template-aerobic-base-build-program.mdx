#### **8-Week Aerobic Base Build Program**

#### **Why Aerobic Base Build Matters**

**Power-Based Progression:** By using power as the objective metric, this program provides a clear, measurable way to track and improve aerobic capacity.

**Structured Development:** The 8-week timeline with bi-weekly power progression offers a systematic approach to building aerobic fitness.

**Balanced Training Mix:** Combining long cyclical work, mixed modalities, and shorter, intense sessions ensures comprehensive aerobic development.

#### **Program Design Insights**

**Bookend Assessments:** The program begins and ends with an aerobic assessment, allowing for clear measurement of progress over the 8 weeks.

**Strategic Progression:** Power increases every other week, with a unique volume increase in week 5 while maintaining power, challenging clients in different ways.

**Varied Session Types:** Three distinct weekly sessions target different aspects of aerobic fitness, ensuring well-rounded development.

#### **Weekly Schedule Breakdown**

1. **Monday: Cyclical Day 1 (Long)**

- Focus: Extended cyclical work to develop endurance through sustained effort
- Benefits: Improves aerobic capacity, fat utilization, and mental toughness

2. **Wednesday: Mixed Cyclical**

- Focus: Blend of modalities to enhance aerobic capabilities across different movements and intensities
- Benefits: Develops versatile fitness, prevents boredom, and improves overall work capacity

3. **Friday: Cyclical Day 2 (Short)**

- Focus: Shorter, more intense cyclical efforts
- Benefits: Improves power output, recovery, and anaerobic threshold

This carefully planned schedule ensures a comprehensive approach to aerobic development, targeting different energy systems and physiological adaptations throughout the week.

#### **The Science Behind Power-Based Aerobic Training**

**Objective Progress Tracking:** Power output provides a more accurate measure of work performed compared to heart rate or perceived exertion, allowing for precise progression.

**Improved Training Specificity:** By focusing on power, athletes can target specific intensity zones more accurately, leading to more effective training adaptations.

**Enhanced Recovery Management:** Power-based training allows for better management of training stress, helping to prevent overtraining and optimize recovery.
