#### WHAT ARE STOREFRONT PAGES?

```
Designing a great program is the easy part. Selling it is a whole other battle…
```

```
This is where the Business Suite feature of CoachRx comes in. It allows you to build a storefront right on the platform, which acts as a hub for everything you sell. 
```

```
As well as showcasing custom sales pages for each product, organizations and individual coaches can build Storefronts that display:
```

- ```
  About/Bio
  ```
- ```
  Coaching staff (and individual coach pages)
  ```
- ```
  FAQs
  ```
- ```
  Testimonials
  ```
- ```
  Contact information
  ```
- ```
  Brand images and videos
  ```

```
The ultimate goal with Storefront Pages is to make sales a lot more hands off and effective for you, and easier for the customer to purchase what you offer, which goes a long way in helping you increase your sales.
```

```
At the very least, it means your clients and potential clients will have access to all your products and services on an app they’re already using, making it more likely they’ll be aware of what you offer and ultimately make a purchase. 
```

```
And for you, it means you can get rid of that clunky website you’re using right now to sell your products and services. 
```

#### HOW COACHES AND CLIENTS CAN BENEFIT FROM STOREFRONTS

```
Consider this: Let’s say you have t-shirts branded with your business logo, but you’re an individual design coach without a brick and mortar facility. Chances are your clients don’t even know you’re selling swag, and if they do, it’s a cumbersome process to take their payment and get them their t-shirt. 
```

```
Switching to Storefront Pages means you can put all the products and services you offer—from t-shirts, to an intro consult, to six-months of individual design, nutrition coaching, templated programs, or supplements—right into your store in CoachRx for your clients to see. So not only will they now be more aware of your offerings, but they can purchase everything on the app.
```

```
Food for thought: Having everything perfectly visible and in one place also provides the coach more opportunity to increase what types of products and services they sell, as now they know everything will be visible to their clients, thus increasing their money-making potential even more (and especially their passive money-making opportunity on products such as templated programs, like a 12-week squat program, or swag). 
```

#### HOW TO USE STOREFRONTS TO SELL YOUR COACHING

<YouTube id="NFpUOP4Jm8M" />

```
Whether you’re an organization or a self-employed, one-person show coach, creating your Storefront Pages couldn’t be simpler.
```

- ```
  Note: If you’re an organization with multiple coaches, each of your coaches has the ability to have their own storefront within your organization, thus providing each coach the ability to sell their own programs, all the while allowing you the ability to monitor each coach’s sales. 
  ```

```
The process involves five simple steps:
```

**Step 1:**

```
Start your free trial with CoachRx to become familiar with the app.
```

**Step 2:**

```
Connect with Stripe (a complete payment platform that integrates with CoachRx).
```

**Step 3:**

```
Input the programs, subscriptions and products you offer.
```

**Step 4:**

```
Build your customizable Storefront Pages (This means you have the ability to design your storefront as you see fit by adding pictures or videos or relevant text to explain to your potential clients and clients what you’re all about, almost like a unique website).
```

**Step 5:**

```
 Embed your storefront in your current site or share direct URLs.
```
