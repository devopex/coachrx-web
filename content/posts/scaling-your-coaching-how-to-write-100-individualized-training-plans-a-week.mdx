As fitness coaches, we've all faced that moment of overwhelm—staring at a screen with dozens of client programs to write and wondering how to maintain quality without working around the clock. Creating truly individualized programs at scale remains one of the greatest challenges in professional coaching.

James FitzGerald addressed this challenge, sharing practical strategies for designing high-quality, individualized training programs efficiently. These insights align with what successful coaches implement to serve large client bases without sacrificing program quality or personal wellbeing.

#### **Strategic Templating: The Foundation of Scalable Program Design**

Professional coaches understand that effective templating doesn't mean generic programming—it means creating intelligent frameworks that can be efficiently customized. Templates serve as thoughtful starting points, not final products.

Effective program templates typically address:

- Training phase appropriateness (accumulation, intensification, competition, deload)
- Experience-based programming (novice, intermediate, advanced)
- Goal-specific frameworks (strength, conditioning, hypertrophy)
- Equipment availability contexts (home gym, commercial facility, minimal equipment)

The key is developing templates that reflect sound programming principles while leaving room for the individual adjustments that make programs truly personalized.

#### **Efficiency Systems for High-Volume Program Design**

Successful coaches implement specific efficiency strategies when managing large client rosters:

**Task Batching**

Dedicating blocks of time to similar program types maximizes cognitive efficiency. This might mean:

- Programming all strength-focused clients in one session
- Handling all program updates for a specific training phase together
- Processing all assessment data in a dedicated block

**Standardized Assessment Protocols**

Comprehensive yet efficient assessment procedures ensure programming decisions are based on objective client data rather than guesswork. This includes:

- Movement screening for fundamental patterns
- Capacity testing appropriate to training goals
- Lifestyle and recovery assessment

**Strategic Exercise Selection**

Maintaining a curated exercise library organized by movement pattern, progression level, and specific adaptations streamlines the selection process while ensuring appropriate exercise choices.

#### **Implementing a Scalable Approach**

To begin scaling your program design process:

1. Audit your current workflow to identify inefficiencies
2. Develop standardized assessment protocols that gather essential data
3. Create foundational templates for common client types
4. Establish a system for regular program updates
5. Implement technology that supports your workflow
