Want to know the secret to making your training and nutrition programs more effective? It starts before your client ever picks up a weight or tracks a macro.

#### The Foundation of Coaching Success

As coaches and fitness professionals, we know that exceptional results don't just come from perfect programming – they come from understanding and optimizing the fundamentals of our clients' daily lives. That's where Lifestyle First programming comes in, and today we're sharing exactly how to implement it using CoachRx.

#### Understanding Lifestyle First Programming

Lifestyle First programming is OPEX's foundational approach to coaching success. Before diving into complex nutrition plans or training programs, we focus on Basic Lifestyle Guidelines (BLGs). This isn't just about tracking habits – it's about understanding that in the OPEX methodology, we progress from Life → Fuel → Person.

You can't effectively personalize nutrition or training until you've mastered these lifestyle basics with your clients.

### Implementation Guide

**1. Access the Lifestyle Feature**

Getting started is simple:

- Open your client's profile
- Click on the left sidebar
- Select "Lifestyle Planning"

**2. Create Your Plan**

Set the foundation for success:

- Design up to 3 prescriptions for a 4-week period
- Start with a timeframe (4 weeks is ideal for checking adherence)
- Add specific lifestyle types (movement, sleep, hydration, etc.)

**3. Set Specific Metrics**

Begin with achievable targets:

- Movement: Start small (e.g., 10-minute walks after meals)
- Sleep: Begin with realistic goals (e.g., screens off 30 minutes before bed)
- Hydration: Set progressive targets (e.g., increase from current intake by 10-20 ounces)

**4. Monitor and Adjust**

Stay engaged with client progress:

- Set notification times for check-ins
- Review compliance daily
- Progress prescriptions every 4 weeks based on adherence

#### Why Lifestyle First Matters

We understand that sustainable results come from a holistic approach. Think about it: nutrition plans affect sleep quality, sleep affects stress management, stress affects digestion – it's all connected.

#### Benefits of Mastering Lifestyle Programming:

- Creates a strong foundation for higher-order nutritional prescriptions
- Prepares clients to progress from inadequate to highest-order food profiles
- Provides concrete data to inform when clients are ready for more personalized nutrition plans
- Supports training programs with lifestyle habits that enhance recovery and adaptation

This isn't just about tracking habits – it's about building the baseline that makes every other aspect of your coaching more effective. Just as we wouldn't prescribe complex training movements without proper mechanics, we shouldn't jump into detailed nutrition plans without lifestyle mastery.

Implementation Strategies for Success

**Start Small**

- Begin with 1-2 lifestyle prescriptions
- Focus on habits that have the highest impact for each client
- Build confidence through early wins

**Progress Methodically**

- Review adherence before adding new prescriptions
- Use data to guide progression
- Celebrate small improvements

**Monitor and Adjust**

- Regular check-ins
- Adjust based on compliance
- Keep communication channels open

#### Pro Implementation Tips

1. Use the notification feature strategically
2. Document client feedback
3. Look for patterns in adherence
4. Build progressive targets
5. Celebrate small wins

Remember

As James FitzGerald says, "The goal over time is always autonomy." Your role is to guide clients towards recognizing where they sit on the continuum and then give them achievable goals that lead to lasting change.
