We're thrilled to announce the latest addition to the CoachRx program library: Weightlifting Base Build - 3 Weeks. This program is a game-changer for coaches committed to enhancing their clients' Olympic weightlifting skills while building a solid strength foundation.

### Why Weightlifting Base Build Matters

**Technique Refinement:** The program focuses on key Olympic lifts such as the Snatch, Clean, and Jerk, ensuring your clients develop proper form and technique in these critical movements.

**Progressive Strength Overload:** Designed with a strategic approach to strength building, this program helps clients steadily increase their lifting capacity.

**Balanced Development:** The well-planned weekly schedule ensures a comprehensive approach to weightlifting, addressing all aspects of the sport.

### Program Design Insights

**Structured Weekly Layout:** The program follows a thoughtfully designed 5-day training week with 2 rest days, allowing for optimal training and recovery.

**Varied Exercise Selection:** Each day focuses on different aspects of Olympic weightlifting, from the main lifts to supportive exercises, ensuring well-rounded development.

**Strategic Progression:** The 3-week structure allows for consistent progress while being short enough to maintain client engagement and allow for regular reassessment.

### Weekly Schedule Breakdown

1. **Monday:** Jerk, Back Squat, Lunge, Squat (speed)

   - Focus: Lower body strength and Jerk technique
2. **Tuesday:** Snatch, Snatch Deadlift, Snatch Pull, Bend

   - Focus: Snatch technique and related strength work
3. **Wednesday:** Rest

   - Allows for recovery and consolidation of skills
4. **Thursday:** Push Press + Overhead Squat, Back Squat, Squat (speed), Push

   - Focus: Overhead strength and stability, lower body power
5. **Friday:** Clean, Clean Deadlift, Clean Pull, Bend

   - Focus: Clean technique and related strength work
6. **Saturday:** Snatch + Overhead Squat, Power Clean + Jerk, Front Squat, Bend

   - Focus: Combining Olympic lifts, building overall weightlifting proficiency
7. **Sunday:** Rest

   - Second recovery day to prepare for the next week
