This episode of *Marketing for Fitness Coaches* walks you through the **five-part system** I’ve been teaching all season, now organized into a **one-page plan** you can stick above your desk, use in your weekly workflow, and finally start to optimize like a coach.

Marketing shouldn’t feel like pressure, it should feel like a practice.

#### Why This One Page Matters

This isn’t just a checklist, it’s a **flywheel**.

Each part of the system feeds the next. When it spins, your marketing gets easier, more effective, and more reflective of your actual coaching values.

No gimmicks. No pressure. Just systems that work because you work them.

#### The 5 Parts of Your Marketing System

Each piece of this plan does a specific job ,and together, they build momentum that lasts.

**1️⃣ Lead Magnet & Nurture**

**Goal:** Turn attention into trust.

Your lead magnet is how you onboard future clients into your world just like an intake form or welcome packet. Keep it:

- Actionable
- Coaching-style
- Simple

Pair it with a 3–5 part email or SMS sequence to keep the conversation going while you coach.

📺 [Episode 4 breaks this down in full.](https://www.notion.so/Ep-16-Blog-21a0519a8d528049ac43d72bcb739095?pvs=21)

**2️⃣ Weekly Content Anchor**

**Goal:** Build a trust library for your future clients.

Choose a long-form format (podcast, YouTube, blog, or newsletter) and publish weekly. This becomes your Client Q&A Library and the source of your weekly short-form content.

Think of it as your **foundational lift**. It builds everything else.

📺 [Episode 5 shows you how to choose and build yours.](https://www.notion.so/Ep-16-Blog-21a0519a8d528049ac43d72bcb739095?pvs=21)

**3️⃣ Lead Flow & Sales Options**

**Goal:** Turn interest into action.

Think of this like your on-ramp protocol:

- A clear sales page
- A consult that feels like coaching
- A nurture flow that makes the next step feel natural

Track what’s working, simplify what’s not.

📺 [Walk the full lead flow path in Episode 6.](https://www.notion.so/Ep-16-Blog-21a0519a8d528049ac43d72bcb739095?pvs=21)

**4️⃣ Referral & Reputation Engine**

**Goal:** Let your results do the talking.

Great coaching creates silent advocates. This step makes it easy for them to speak up:

- Ask for testimonials
- Automate review requests
- Build your local (and digital) “3-mile hero” presence

📺 [Strategize your referral engine in Episode 7.](https://www.notion.so/Ep-16-Blog-21a0519a8d528049ac43d72bcb739095?pvs=21)

**5️⃣ Daily Micro-Marketing Ritual**

**Goal:** Stay top of mind without burning out.

Clip from your weekly anchor. Share insights, wins, and value in daily doses. Think of this like your warm-up, short, intentional, and momentum-building.

📺 [Master your micro-marketing in Episode 10.](https://www.notion.so/Ep-16-Blog-21a0519a8d528049ac43d72bcb739095?pvs=21)

#### Snapshot: The One-Page Plan Checklist

Stick this on your wall or desktop and make it a working practice.
