Before you start
What counts as a client? +

Active clients only. Archiving a client removes them from your count, so you never pay for someone you have stopped coaching.

What happens when I outgrow my plan? +

You move up when you add the client that crosses the line, prorated. No sales call.

What happens after the 14 days? +

Your card is charged on day 15 for the plan matching your active client count. Cancel any time before then and you are not charged.

Is there a contract? +

No. Monthly is month to month. Annual is a twelve-month term, paid in full up front, at the lower rate.

What if it doesn't work out? +

Export your clients, workouts and reporting at any time, or pause the subscription instead of cancelling.
