04 · The system

Coaching isn't five jobs. It shouldn't be five tools.

Most coaches run a practice across a programming app, a spreadsheet, a messaging thread, a payment processor, and a notes doc. The work still gets done. The context is what gets lost.

In CoachRx, what you learn in an assessment is there when you design. What a client logs is there before your next conversation. One system, so every decision has the full picture behind it.

Text is the least of what you can send.

Loom video, voice notes, GIFs, emojis, and threaded messages all sit inside the client's file, with several conversations open at once when you are working through the roster. Show a correction instead of describing it. Let a client hear your tone on a hard week. Every conversation stays connected to the work it is about.

Check in before they check out.

Set the cadence and the questions once — start from a provided template or build the check-in fully custom — and every client is prompted to report how the week actually went. Answers land next to their compliance and trend data, so you read the response with the context already in front of you.

The habit that protects retention.

Set a communication goal for your organization, for a coach, or for a single client, and CoachRx counts every comment, message, and consultation — we call them touchpoints — against it. Each client carries a simple weekly status: reached, or not yet. It resets Monday, so the clients you have not spoken to surface while there is still week left to fix it.

14 · FAQ

Questions coaches ask before they start.

Do I have to use all of it?+

No. Most coaches start with program design and the client app, then bring in assessments, lifestyle, and the business tools as they need them. Everything is available from day one, but nothing is mandatory.

Can I bring my own programs and movements?+

Yes. Upload your own movement videos into your Coach Exercise Library, save your program structures for reuse, and keep your warm-ups, cool-downs, and conditioning pieces as templates. The OPEX libraries are there when you want a starting point, not a requirement.

What does it cost?+

Pricing scales with the number of clients you manage, and every plan includes every feature. See plans and pricing for the current bands.

Will this make my coaching feel systematic?+

The opposite. CoachRx handles the mechanics, billing, tracking, and surfacing what needs your attention, so that more of your week goes into the conversations only you can have.

Do my clients need a separate app?+

No. Coaches and clients use the same app, with the experience each of them needs. One download, one login.

Can I import from another platform?+

Yes. Bring your whole roster over with one upload, or let our transition team handle the migration for you. There's no charge for it.

What if it doesn't work out?+

Your data is yours. You can export your clients, workouts, and reporting at any time, and if you need a break rather than an exit you can pause your subscription instead of cancelling it.
