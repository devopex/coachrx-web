Program library search

Search now spans the CoachRx Index, your own exercise library, and every saved template in a single query. Results are grouped by source, and selecting one drops it straight into the day you are building.

  • One search field across all three libraries, with keyboard navigation.
  • Filters for equipment, pattern, and the libraries you want included.
  • Recent selections surface first while you are building a program.

Improvements

Program design

  • Copying a week now carries attached notes and lifestyle prescriptions.
  • Tempo fields accept ranges.

Client app

  • Faster session load on older Android devices.
  • Progress pictures can be captured in-app without leaving the day view.

Fixes
