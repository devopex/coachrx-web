Solo coaches, coaching teams and large organizations all run on CoachRx. The difference is what you need it to do on a Tuesday.

Every client gets their own program, which means every client is a separate pile of decisions and you are the only place they connect.

Assessment, program, nutrition and lifestyle sit on one calendar per client, so individual design stops meaning individual chaos. Build a week for one person in a single pass instead of four.

What changes
01 One client, one calendarTraining, nutrition and lifestyle prescribed in the same place, not three tools.
02 Assessment drives the programWhat you found in the consult sits beside the block you are writing.
03 Reuse the thinking, not the templateProgressions carry across clients without flattening anyone into a template.

You never see your clients in person, so everything you know about them arrives through a screen.

Structured check-ins, video review and a branded app, so a remote practice still feels like coaching rather than a file transfer.

What changes
01 Structured check-ins instead of chased messagesThe same questions every week, answered before you open the thread.
02 Video review in the workout, not the inboxThey film the lift, you comment on the set it belongs to.
03 A branded app in their pocketYour name and colors, so the work feels like yours and not like rented software.

You are running sessions all day and writing programs at night, in a spreadsheet you have rebuilt four times.

Write the session once, deliver it to the phone in their hand, and see whether it actually happened before they walk back in.

What changes
01 The program leaves the spreadsheetEvery client's plan lives in one place you can open between sessions.
02 They log it, you see itLoads, reps and how it felt, in your hands before the next session starts.
03 Billing sits with the coachingPayments and invoices run from the same system, not a separate app.

You are managing squads, testing windows and return to play, and a spreadsheet is holding all of it together.

Test data, group templates and individual adjustment in one system, so a squad stays a squad without every athlete getting the same session.

What changes
01 Assessment data drives the blockTest results sit with the athlete and shape what comes next.
02 Write once for the squad, adjust per athleteGroup structure, individual load, without rewriting the block.
03 Load and readiness on one timelineTraining load, sleep and wellness beside the sessions that caused them.

Your clients log their food in one app and you write their plan in another, so nothing ever lines up.

Calories, macros and habits are prescribed on the same calendar as their training, and their intake syncs in from wherever they already log it.

What changes
01 Nutrition on the same calendar as everything elsePrescribed beside training and lifestyle, not in a separate document.
02 Intake syncs from where they already logCronometer, MyFitnessPal, FatSecret and MacrosFirst come in automatically.
03 Compliance is one number, not fourFood, training and lifestyle adherence in the same trend view.

You are running group classes and individual clients out of the same building, on two different systems.

Group programming and individual design come off one calendar. Members, billing and progress live in one place, so the front desk is not running a second system.

What changes
01 One calendar, both sides of the businessClass programming and individual design stop living in separate tools.
